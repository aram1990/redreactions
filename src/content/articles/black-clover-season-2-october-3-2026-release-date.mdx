Black Clover Season 2 now has an exact premiere date. The anime returns to Japanese television on **October 3, 2026 at 11:00 p.m. JST** on TV Tokyo and its affiliated stations, more than five years after the original 170-episode run ended. Crunchyroll has confirmed it will stream Season 2 internationally, though an exact international release time hasn't been announced.

## What Today's Announcement Actually Changed

Season 2 has been confirmed since July 2025, and an October 2026 broadcast window has been public since June 2026. What Black Clover's official site added on September 7 is the specific date, the specific broadcast time, and the specific ending theme — the details that turn "sometime in October" into an actual date on a calendar. The site also listed four Japanese platforms carrying an early stream ahead of the TV Tokyo broadcast: Anime Times, Lemino, U-NEXT, and Anime Hōdai, with further details on timing still to come.

## Japan's Broadcast Time Isn't a Global Release Time

Crunchyroll confirmed months ago that it holds the international rights outside Asia, and it's hosting a one-night theatrical sneak peek of the premiere in select U.S. theaters on September 21. But nothing announced so far pins an exact simulcast time for Crunchyroll's own rollout. Treat "11:00 p.m. JST on TV Tokyo" as the confirmed Japanese broadcast slot, not as when the episode becomes available everywhere else.

## More Than Five Years Off the Air

Black Clover's original TV run began in 2017 and closed out at 170 episodes in March 2021. The franchise didn't disappear in between — 2023 brought the theatrical film *Black Clover: Sword of the Wizard King*, also directed by Ayataka Tanemura, who returns for Season 2 alongside Studio Pierrot. That continuity matters here: this isn't a new studio or director inheriting someone else's unfinished story, but the same creative team picking the series back up.

Series regulars are also confirmed returning, including Gakuto Kajiwara as Asta, Nobunaga Shimazaki as Yuno, Junichi Suwabe as Yami, Kana Yuki as Noelle, and Hiro Shimono as Nacht.

## Where the Story Picks Up

Season 1 ended mid-arc, well short of where Yuki Tabata's manga eventually finished. That manga concluded its run in spring 2026 after eleven years, closing out Asta and Yuno's story. Black Clover's official announcement hasn't said how much of that remaining story Season 2 covers or whether it's positioned as the anime's final stretch — the manga wrapping up doesn't by itself confirm how far this season goes, so there's no reason to expect it closes out the whole series in one run.

## What's Still Unconfirmed

TV Tokyo hasn't announced an episode count, and neither Crunchyroll nor any Japanese streamer has published exact rollout times beyond the broadcast slot itself. A dub schedule, the full extent of story covered this season, and territory-by-territory availability outside Crunchyroll's regions also remain unannounced.
