**Muramasa: Revenant Blades** launches **February 4, 2027** on PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch and PC via Steam. Developer Vanillaware and publisher Marvelous are positioning it as the definitive way to play the 2D action-RPG: it combines the 2009 Wii original, *Muramasa: The Demon Blade*, with its PS Vita-era *Genroku Legends* expansion into a single package, rebuilt for current hardware.

## What's actually different from the Wii and Vita versions

*Muramasa: The Demon Blade* released on Wii in 2009 with two playable characters, Kisuke and Momohime. The PS Vita follow-up, *Muramasa: Rebirth*, added the *Genroku Legends* DLC campaign with additional protagonists. *Revenant Blades* is Vanillaware and Marvelous's own framing of what this release is: those two eras "combined and further refined into a single, complete experience," with **six protagonists playable from the start** rather than split across a base game and paid DLC.

The studio also confirms visual and mechanical updates on top of that consolidation — Vanillaware's 2D character art has been "revitalized in gorgeous high-definition" and upgraded to support **4K resolution on modern platforms**, and the game ships with **updated controls**, including parrying and deflection options that weren't part of the original combat design.

## Combat and the skill system

Combat centers on the same real-time sword-swapping system the series is known for, now built around **over 100 forgeable swords**, each carrying its own Secret Arts. Marvelous describes a skill system where players assign **up to three mastered skills for both ground and aerial combat**, letting characters mix single strikes, wide-area attacks and long-range Secret Arts rather than relying on one fixed moveset. A spirit gauge governs weapon-switching between a character's two equipped blades, and PlayStation's own material confirms seamless movement between ground and air combos.

For players who want the unmodified original, the release also includes a way to play through *Muramasa: The Demon Blade* and *Genroku Legends* largely as they originally shipped, with enhanced graphics rather than the reworked modern systems — outlets have described this bundled option under slightly different names, so Red Reactions isn't treating any single label as the confirmed official term until Marvelous standardizes it.

## English voiceover, for the first time

*Revenant Blades* adds **all-new English voice acting** across its full cast — something neither the Wii nor Vita versions had. Marvelous pairs that with "all-new English localization," suggesting more than just a new audio track; expect revised script and text alongside the voice work.

## Platforms and the Switch 2 edition

*Revenant Blades* releases day-and-date on PS5, Switch 2, Switch and PC via Steam — notably Vanillaware's first PC release since 2006's *Fantasy Earth Zero*. On Switch 2 specifically, the physical release is confirmed to ship as a **full game on the cartridge**, not a game-key card requiring a download.

## Editions and pricing

The **standard edition** is digital-or-physical at $49.99 on PS5 and Switch, and $59.99 on Switch 2. The **limited edition** runs $89.99 on PS5/Switch and $99.99 on Switch 2, and includes a 120-plus-page Vanillaware artbook, a Basiscape soundtrack CD, and a decorative tabletop byōbu folding screen, packaged in box art featuring Kisuke and Momohime.

## What's not confirmed

Marvelous and PlayStation's announcements don't mention a frame rate target, HDR support, ray tracing, or any performance-mode options for the console versions. There's also no confirmed save-transfer, cross-save or cross-buy support between platforms, and no DLC has been announced beyond what's already bundled into the base release. Treat any of those as unconfirmed until Vanillaware or Marvelous says otherwise.
