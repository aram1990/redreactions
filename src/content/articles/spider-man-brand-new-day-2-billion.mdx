*Spider-Man: Brand New Day* has crossed **$2 billion worldwide**, becoming only the eighth film in history to reach the milestone and the second-fastest to get there.

According to Sunday box-office estimates, Tom Holland's latest Spider-Man film has reached approximately **$2.022 billion globally**, including **$785.8 million** in the U.S. and Canada and **$1.236 billion** internationally.

The Associated Press says *Brand New Day* reached $2 billion in about 18 days. Only *Avengers: Endgame* did it faster, hitting the mark in 11 days in 2019.

## Still No. 1

The Sony Pictures release stayed at the top of the North American box office for a third consecutive weekend, adding an estimated **$70 million**.

That pushed its domestic total to $785.8 million, currently the **fourth-highest domestic gross ever** without adjusting for inflation. Deadline reports that the $70 million haul is also the **second-biggest third weekend in domestic box-office history**, behind *Star Wars: The Force Awakens*.

International markets added another **$118.7 million** during the weekend, helping push the film beyond the $2 billion barrier.

## A New Spider-Man Record

The milestone takes *Brand New Day* past *Spider-Man: No Way Home*, which finished above $1.9 billion, making *Brand New Day* the highest-grossing Spider-Man movie to date and Sony's biggest worldwide release.

[Its record run started immediately](/articles/spider-man-brand-new-day-700-million-box-office/). Sony's final opening-weekend figures put the film at approximately **$360.1 million domestically**, narrowly beating *Avengers: Endgame's* previous unadjusted opening record.

The numbers are still moving. Sunday's totals are studio estimates, and final weekend figures are expected Monday, so the exact $2.022 billion total may shift slightly.
