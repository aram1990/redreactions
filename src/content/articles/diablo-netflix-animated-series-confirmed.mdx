Blizzard confirmed at BlizzCon 2026 that *Diablo* is heading to Netflix as an animated series. That's the real, official news. Almost everything past that — when, who's making it, which game it draws from — is still unknown.

## What Blizzard actually announced

Blizzard's own wording, from its BlizzCon 2026 materials: "After decades of bringing entire universes to life through our games, we're partnering with some creative powerhouses to tell even more stories beyond gaming across new screens. A new animated series set in the world of Diablo is in development with Netflix." Blizzard president Johanna Faries delivered the news on stage, telling the crowd directly: "We are thrilled to share that we are in development on a new animated series for Diablo with Netflix." Both statements say the same thing: this is a real, confirmed project, and it's explicitly **animated**, not live-action. Netflix has not yet published its own separate announcement or project page.

## What "set in the world of Diablo" does and doesn't tell us

That phrase confirms the Sanctuary setting and nothing more specific. Blizzard has not said the series adapts *Diablo I*, *II*, *III*, *IV*, or the newly announced *Diablo V* — nor has it described an original story disconnected from any specific game. Don't assume this ties into *Diablo V*'s own storyline just because both were announced at the same event; Blizzard has kept the two projects entirely separate in its own materials.

## The details that remain unknown

As of this announcement, Blizzard and Netflix have not revealed:

- A release date or release window
- An animation studio
- A showrunner, writer or director
- Any cast, voice or otherwise
- An episode count or season order
- Which characters, era or storyline the series covers

Faries also mentioned, in the same appearance, that adaptations of other Blizzard franchises — Overwatch and Warcraft among them — "could" happen down the line. That's a tease about future possibilities, not an announcement of any specific project; treat it as exactly that level of confirmed.

## Why this is being called "back in development"

This isn't Netflix and Blizzard's first attempt at a *Diablo* adaptation. Netflix was reportedly working on anime-style adaptations of both *Diablo* and *Overwatch* back in 2018–2019, with Nick van Dyk — then co-president of Activision Blizzard Studios — confirming the *Diablo* project had reached pre-production. Those plans stalled after Activision Blizzard sued Netflix over the alleged recruitment of its then-CFO, Spencer Neumann, a legal dispute that damaged the companies' working relationship and left both projects shelved. This new BlizzCon 2026 confirmation is the first sign since then that a Netflix *Diablo* project is actively moving forward again — which is the honest way to describe it, rather than treating it as a brand-new idea with no history.

## How this differs from Diablo V

Red Reactions has separately covered [Diablo V's first confirmed classes](/articles/diablo-v-classes-plague-knight-blizzcon-guide/), also announced at BlizzCon 2026. That's a mainline game, targeting a Spring 2029 release, with confirmed classes and gameplay systems. The Netflix series is a different medium entirely — animated television, not a game — and Blizzard has given no indication the two projects share a story, timeline, or release schedule. Searching for one doesn't get you information about the other.

## The bottom line

Diablo's Netflix series is real, it's animated, and it's actively in development after years of a prior attempt sitting dormant. That's the entire confirmed picture right now. Release date, cast, studio, story — all of it is still ahead of us, and Blizzard has said only that more details will come "when the team is ready."
