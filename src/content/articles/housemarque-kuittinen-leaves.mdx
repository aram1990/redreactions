Ilari Kuittinen, the co-founder of Housemarque, is leaving the Finnish development studio after 31 years. However, this is not an abrupt departure that leaves an empty leadership position. Sami "Haxu" Hakala already took over as managing director on July 1, 2026, and Kuittinen has spent the last two months supporting the transition before his final day on August 31, 2026.

There is currently no announced evidence that the change alters Housemarque’s strategy, causes development disruption, or affects any unannounced projects. Kuittinen explicitly noted that "Housemarque’s strategy and creative vision are in exceptional hands."

### A 33-Year Journey Through Gaming History

Kuittinen spent 33 years continuously working in games. The journey began in 1993 with his earlier work at Terramarque. By 1995, he and Harri Tikkanen established Housemarque, forging an identity built on tight arcade and action mechanics.

That arcade focus led to defining early titles and hits like *Super Stardust HD* and *Resogun*. The studio remained independent for 26 years, eventually achieving a major AAA breakthrough with the PlayStation 5 exclusive *Returnal*. That success solidified their relationship with Sony Interactive Entertainment, which acquired Housemarque in 2021. The studio then followed up with *Saros*, which released in late April 2026.

### Who is Sami Hakala?

Sami "Haxu" Hakala is not a new face brought in by Sony. He is a veteran of the studio who served as deputy managing director before officially assuming the managing director role on July 1. Kuittinen remained at Housemarque through the end of August specifically to support the leadership handover and ensure operational continuity.

As for Kuittinen, while he is taking a well-deserved break starting in September, he explicitly stated that he expects to stay connected to the games industry and may later mentor or consult. It is a pause rather than a confirmed permanent retirement.
