*Stranger Things: Tales From '85* Season 2 premieres **September 17, 2026** on Netflix, with **eight** roughly 30-minute episodes. It's the animated series' second run, and it's set during the winter of 1985 — placing it between the live-action show's Season 2 (1984) and Season 3 (1985), in the gap the flagship series never actually shows on screen.

## What Tales From '85 Actually Is

*Tales From '85* is a CG-animated spin-off, not a retelling of the live-action show. It follows the same core kids — Eleven, Mike, Will, Dustin, Lucas and Max — reorganized as the **Hawkins Investigators Club (HIC)**, investigating paranormal mysteries in Hawkins with a lighter, more adventure-driven tone than its parent series. Showrunner Eric Robles leads the project. Netflix has positioned it as a more accessible, family-friendly companion to the main show, though it still carries a TV-PG rating and isn't aimed at viewers with zero context — its cast, setting and recurring threats only make full sense with some familiarity with the live-action series.

## Where Season 2 Picks Up

Season 2 follows directly from Season 1's cliffhanger, which ended with a Demogorgon emerging from the corpse of the Upside Down's "Queen." Netflix's official synopsis for the new season: with Valentine's Day approaching, mysterious ghostly apparitions and a swarm of "hate sprites" start wreaking havoc around Hawkins, and the HIC has to uncover what's causing it before a new gate to the Upside Down tears open. Beyond that setup, Netflix hasn't detailed how the season resolves or what the apparitions and hate sprites actually are.

## Who's Returning

The HIC's core lineup returns with the same voice cast: Brooklyn Davey Norstedt as Eleven, Luca Diaz as Mike Wheeler, Benjamin Plessala as Will Byers, Braxton Quinney as Dustin Henderson, Elisha "EJ" Williams as Lucas Sinclair, and Jolie Hoang-Rappaport as Max Mayfield. Brett Gipson returns as Jim Hopper, and Jeremy Jordan returns voicing Steve Harrington. Netflix's own materials also list Odessa A'Zion voicing Nikki Baxter, a character introduced as part of the HIC roster.

## New Ground, Including a Hawkins Mine

Familiar locations return — Hawkins Middle School and the Palace Arcade among them — but Robles has specifically flagged new territory for Season 2. His own description: "we're going to go places that we haven't been to before... There's a mine in Hawkins that we haven't explored yet." That's the extent of what's confirmed about the new setting; Netflix hasn't detailed what the HIC finds there or how it connects to the season's central mystery.

## Do You Need to Watch the Live-Action Show First?

Partially. *Tales From '85* doesn't require encyclopedic knowledge of all five live-action seasons, but Season 2 specifically assumes you've seen Season 1 of the animated series — it opens directly from that finale's cliffhanger. Beyond that, its characters, Hawkins setting, and recurring concepts like the Upside Down and the Demogorgon are drawn straight from the live-action show, so newcomers will follow the plot more easily with at least passing familiarity with the main series. It's built to be a gentler entry point than the flagship show, not a fully standalone story.

## Timeline: Where This Sits in Stranger Things Continuity

*Tales From '85* is set in the specific stretch of time between the live-action show's Season 2 (which takes place in 1984) and Season 3 (summer 1985) — a period the main series never dramatized on screen. That placement lets the animated series tell new stories about the same characters without touching events the live-action show has already covered, and without contradicting where Season 3 picks those characters up. Netflix hasn't stated that anything in *Tales From '85* is treated as mandatory viewing for understanding the live-action seasons — it fills a gap in the timeline rather than rewriting it.

## What We Still Don't Know

Netflix hasn't explained what the ghostly apparitions or hate sprites actually are, who or what is trying to open the new gate, or how directly this season's mystery connects to Vecna's larger presence in the Upside Down. Those questions are what the eight episodes are built to answer.
