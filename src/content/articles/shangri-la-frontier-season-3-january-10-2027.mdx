*Shangri-La Frontier* Season 3 now has an exact Japanese premiere date. The official anime website confirmed on September 16 that the series returns **January 10, 2027**, airing Sundays at 5:00 p.m. JST on MBS, TBS and 28 affiliated stations nationwide. The same announcement confirmed the season will run for **two consecutive cours** and revealed a new key visual carrying the tagline "混沌が爆ぜる" ("Chaos explodes").

## What "Two Consecutive Cours" Actually Confirms

A cour is a standard Japanese broadcast block, typically covering a season of roughly three months. Confirming "two consecutive cours" means Season 3 is set up to air continuously across two of those blocks back-to-back, rather than splitting into a two-part release with a mid-season break — the kind of structure some anime use to space out production.

What it does **not** confirm is an exact episode count. Two cours commonly line up with 24 to 26 episodes, but the official site hasn't published a specific number, so any exact figure circulating elsewhere isn't sourced to this announcement.

## The New Key Visual and What It Shows

The newly released key visual features protagonist Sunraku alongside returning cast members, rendered in the same style established across the franchise's prior key art. Framed around the season's "Chaos explodes" tagline, it's consistent with the story direction the official site describes for Season 3: Sunraku steps outside the Shangri-La Frontier game itself to compete in a new VR fighting game called **Galaxia Heroes: Chaos**, entering the "Global Game Competition" tournament, where he and his teammates face the American pro team Star Rain.

That's a meaningful setting shift from the first two seasons, which stayed inside the original Shangri-La Frontier game world. The official material frames Galaxia Heroes as built on the Shangri-La Frontier game engine within the story's fiction, rather than an unrelated spin-off game.

## Broadcast Confirmed, International Streaming Still Open

Everything above is specific to the Japanese TV broadcast. Internationally, Crunchyroll announced in August, during a panel at AnimagiC 2026, that it has licensed Shangri-La Frontier Season 3 — confirming a streaming partner exists for audiences outside Japan. What hasn't been announced alongside today's premiere-date news is an exact international release schedule, simulcast timing relative to the Japanese broadcast, or whether every territory gets Season 3 on the same day it airs in Japan. Season 3 streaming on Crunchyroll the way Seasons 1 and 2 did is a reasonable expectation given the license, but the exact rollout details are still Crunchyroll's to confirm.

## Where This Picks Up From Season 2

Season 2 closed out its own arc within the Shangri-La Frontier game, and the official announcement doesn't detail how directly Season 3's opening episodes connect back to those events beyond the confirmed shift to the Galaxia Heroes tournament setting. Returning cast and staff for Season 3 haven't been detailed in this announcement, so nothing beyond the confirmed setting and broadcast details should be treated as locked in yet.

Shangri-La Frontier isn't the only franchise with a firm January 2027 return already on the books — [The World's Finest Assassin Season 2 is also set for that window](/articles/worlds-finest-assassin-season-2-january-2027/), giving the season an early anchor point even before the rest of the winter lineup fills in.

## What's Still Unconfirmed

No exact episode count, no confirmed returning voice cast or staff beyond what's implied by continuity, and no specific international streaming date have been announced. Treat January 10, 2027 and the two-cour broadcast structure as the confirmed facts here — everything else about how Season 3 plays out remains to be announced by the official site or Crunchyroll.
