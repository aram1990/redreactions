*Slow Horses* Season 6 is now streaming globally on Apple TV, having premiered **Wednesday, September 16, 2026**, with six episodes releasing weekly through an **October 21 finale**. Here's the practical version of what to know — what's actually confirmed, what the trailer revealed, and how much of Season 5 you need to remember.

> **Spoiler warning:** This guide discusses confirmed events through Season 5, clearly labeled. It does not recap or reveal anything from Season 6's aired episodes, and nothing from Mick Herron's novels is presented as confirmed for the show beyond what Apple has officially stated.

## Release schedule and finale date

Episode 1, "Circle of Life," premiered September 16 and is now streaming. One new episode releases weekly after that, with the season closing on its sixth and final episode on **October 21, 2026**. This is a six-episode season, consistent with the show's usual format.

## The two books behind this season

Season 6 adapts **two** of Mick Herron's novels at once — *Joe Country* and *Slough House*, the sixth and seventh books in his Slough House series. That's notable: most prior seasons have drawn on a single novel. Apple's official synopsis frames the season around the team being on the run, with Diana Taverner pulling them into what the studio calls "a fatally high-stakes game of retaliation and revenge." Treat that framing as the confirmed shape of the season — the novels exist as source material, not as a preview of exactly how the show will adapt them scene for scene.

## What you need to remember from Season 5

**Spoilers for Season 5 follow.** By the end of Season 5, Diana Taverner has become First Desk — MI5's top position — after Lamb effectively forced out her predecessor, Claude Whelan. River Cartwright saved Whelan during the finale and hoped it would finally earn him a ticket back to the Park (MI5's main headquarters, as opposed to the disgraced backwater of Slough House). It didn't happen: Taverner, who has her own history and dislike of River, declines to bring him back, and Lamb — in his usual mix of self-interest and something like protectiveness — keeps River at Slough House rather than letting him go. Season 5 ends on a forward-looking note: a glimpse of River finally coming face to face with his estranged father, **Frank Harkness**, the mercenary who served as Season 4's main antagonist and hadn't been seen since.

That confrontation is exactly what Season 6's returning cast list confirms is coming: Hugo Weaving is back as Frank Harkness for the first time since that Season 4 finale.

## Sid Baker's return, and what's actually confirmed about it

Here's the biggest name in the Season 6 trailer, and it needs the context to land: Sidonie "Sid" Baker, played by Olivia Cooke, was shot in the head and believed dead during Season 1. The Season 6 trailer ends with her reappearing alive — Apple's own announcement confirms plainly that "Olivia Cooke will reprise her role as Sidonie 'Sid' Baker in the sixth season." What isn't confirmed anywhere official yet is how she survived, where she's been for five seasons, or what she wants now. Treat "Sid is alive and returning" as fact; treat any explanation for it as something the season itself hasn't answered yet.

## Who else is back

Apple's official cast list for Season 6: Gary Oldman (Jackson Lamb), Kristin Scott Thomas, Jack Lowden, Saskia Reeves, Christopher Chung, Aimee-Ffion Edwards, Rosalind Eleazar, Joanna Scanlan, Samuel West, Ruth Bradley, Tom Brooke, Jonathan Pryce, Hugo Weaving, and newcomer Lenny Rush.

## What Apple has confirmed about the new threat

The official framing is specific but not detailed: the Slough House team is being hunted, and Diana Taverner is using the crisis as leverage for her own game of "retaliation and revenge." Apple hasn't named who's actually doing the hunting beyond what the trailer itself shows, and hasn't confirmed how directly Frank Harkness's return connects to that threat. Both are reasonable things to expect the premiere to clarify — neither is confirmed yet.

## Do you need to rewatch earlier seasons?

Not the whole thing. The two threads worth having fresh going in are River's stalled status at Slough House under Taverner's new leadership, and the fact that Frank Harkness — not seen since Season 4 — is confirmed to return. Sid Baker's history as a Season 1 casualty is useful context, but the show itself will almost certainly re-establish it for anyone who doesn't remember.

## Slow Horses is already renewed through Season 8

Apple confirmed on September 15, 2026 — one day before Season 6 premiered — that *Slow Horses* has been renewed for an eighth season, on top of Season 7, which Apple had already greenlit back in July 2025. So anyone watching Season 6 now knows going in that the show is secured for at least two more seasons after this one.

Season 7 adapts *Bad Actors*, the eighth Mick Herron novel, and follows Lamb and the Slough House team tracking down a mole inside the British government; it already finished filming, though Apple hasn't given it a premiere date beyond a general expectation of 2027.

Season 8 is a six-episode adaptation of *Clown Town*, the ninth novel in Herron's Slough House series. Apple's official setup for it: "Jackson Lamb and Diana Taverner face off in their most explosive confrontation yet. When a botched attempt to blackmail the Park puts Taverner on the offensive, it is up to Lamb to unravel one of MI5's darkest secrets and put an end to it once and for all." Gary Oldman returns as Jackson Lamb and Kristin Scott Thomas returns as Diana Taverner. Ben Vanstone adapts the novel and executive produces, and Adam Randall — who directed Season 4 and won an Emmy for it — returns to direct.

Apple has not announced a Season 8 premiere date. Treat that logline as the confirmed shape of the season, not a preview of how Season 6 or Season 7 gets the show there — nothing here implies how either of those seasons resolves.

## The bottom line

Six episodes, one per week, through an October 21 finale, adapting *Joe Country* and *Slough House* at once. The season picks up with River stuck exactly where Season 5 left him, brings back Frank Harkness for the first time in two seasons, and resurrects a character the show killed off in its very first season — all fact, none of it yet explained. That's the setup Apple has confirmed; the "why" is the season's job. And however that plays out, Apple has already committed to telling this story through at least two more seasons after it.
