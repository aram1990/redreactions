*One Piece* is heading back to theaters — twice.

The franchise announced **ONE PIECE FILM GOD VALLEY** for **summer 2027 in Japan**, followed by **ONE PIECE FILM BAAD** in **2029**. Both were revealed on August 23 immediately after the main stage at **ONE PIECE DAY '26** at Makuhari Messe in Chiba.

The [official *One Piece* announcement](https://one-piece.com/news/81811/index.html) gives the two release windows but stops well short of revealing full plots, casts or staff. That matters, especially for *God Valley*: the title is loaded with manga history, but the title alone should not be treated as confirmation of exactly how much of that story the film will adapt.

## Why One Piece Film God Valley is such a big title

*God Valley* is the first new *One Piece* theatrical film since ***One Piece Film Red*** opened in Japan in August 2022. The timing also places it directly inside the manga's **30th anniversary year**. Eiichiro Oda's series began serialization in *Weekly Shonen Jump* on July 22, 1997, making July 22, 2027 the official 30-year milestone.

The name itself is enough to make this announcement unusually important. God Valley is tied to one of the most consequential incidents in *One Piece* history, involving figures such as **Gol D. Roger, Monkey D. Garp and Rocks D. Xebec**. Famitsu notes that the newly released super teaser points toward those central figures, but the announcement still does not provide a conventional synopsis or spell out the film's full narrative scope.

That distinction is worth keeping. There is a big difference between a movie being titled *God Valley* and the studio already confirming every manga event, character or revelation that fans associate with the incident.

For now, there is **no exact Japanese release date beyond summer 2027**, and no international theatrical date has been announced.

## One Piece Film BAAD is still a mystery

The second announcement is even harder to read.

**ONE PIECE FILM BAAD** is scheduled for **2029**, the same year the TV anime celebrates its own 30th anniversary. The anime premiered in Japan on October 20, 1999, putting its 30-year anniversary on October 20, 2029.

The most concrete creative detail revealed so far is the title image itself. Japanese coverage from [ORICON News](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2475579/full/) and [Cinema Today](https://www.cinematoday.jp/news/N0156448) reports that **Eiichiro Oda personally drew the BAAD title lettering** shown during the announcement.

What "BAAD" means has not been explained. The lettering gives the final **D** unusual visual emphasis, but turning that into a story theory would be speculation at this stage. There is no announced synopsis, director, cast or stated connection between *BAAD* and *God Valley* beyond the fact that both films were revealed together.

## Five years after Film Red

The double announcement ends a long theatrical gap for the franchise. *Film Red* was released in Japan on August 6, 2022 and became the biggest *One Piece* movie at the Japanese box office, finishing above **¥20 billion** domestically after its original run and later encore screenings.

One detail in some early coverage is worth correcting: *Film Red* was officially described by Toei Animation as the franchise's **15th film**. That means *God Valley* and *BAAD* would follow as the **16th and 17th theatrical films** if the numbering continues normally — not the 15th and 16th.

For now, the useful facts are simpler than the speculation: *God Valley* arrives in Japan in summer 2027, *BAAD* follows in 2029, and neither film has an announced international release date yet.

The titles alone give *One Piece* fans plenty to argue about. The actual story details can wait until Toei and the official *One Piece* team reveal them.
