Konami Press Start aired **September 3**, and the biggest surprise was a brand-new reveal: *Rhapsody in Scarlet*, a 1920s New York action-adventure nobody had seen before. Alongside it, Konami formally unveiled *PROJECT ZIRCON*, expanded what's known about *Rev. NOiR*, and showed new material from *Silent Hill: Townfall*, *Castlevania: Belmont's Curse*, *KONAMI Wai Wai World Craft* and *eFootball: Kick-Off!*.

Before the show, Konami had confirmed *Silent Hill: Townfall*, *Castlevania: Belmont's Curse* and *Rev. NOiR* as highlighted games, alongside *eFootball: Kick-Off!* and a project then known as Project Zircoin. The broadcast ran roughly 30 minutes and mixed new gameplay footage with video messages from the games' creators and development teams.

## What Konami Actually Announced

### Rhapsody in Scarlet

Konami's newly revealed original action-adventure is set in **1920s New York** and follows **Ellie Myers**, an aspiring jazz singer and young magus. Combat combines melee, ranged and area-of-effect attacks with magick and mystical beast abilities in a combo-based system. It's confirmed for **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC via Steam**, with a release date still **to be announced**. The English voice cast pairs Cherami Leigh as Ellie and Hannah Grace as Ilya, with Akari Kito and Saori Onishi voicing the same roles in Japanese, and the theme song comes from SOIL & "PIMP" SESSIONS with singer-songwriter Emi Meyer.

### PROJECT ZIRCON

Konami formally unveiled PROJECT ZIRCON as a new deck-building roguelike card game, built around alternating strategy and admin phases as players expand territory, acquire cards and facilities, and work through branching scenarios toward multiple endings. Steam wishlists are open now; Konami hasn't announced platforms beyond that or a release date.

### Rev. NOiR

Rev. NOiR now has a release year: **2027**. Konami has confirmed **Xbox Series X|S and Steam** alongside the previously announced PlayStation 5 version. New Story and Battle trailers introduced protagonists **Asch** and **Finé**, set against a world-threatening catastrophe called **lightfall**, with strategic-action combat built around Astral Sigils and Kardieoses. No specific 2027 release window has been given.

### Silent Hill: Townfall

<img src="/images/articles/konami-press-start-2026-silent-hill-townfall-castlevania/silent-hill-townfall-official-art.jpg" alt="Official key art for Silent Hill: Townfall showing the protagonist walking through a foggy street." loading="lazy" />

*Silent Hill: Townfall launches September 24 on PS5, Steam and Epic Games Store. Credit: Konami Digital Entertainment / Annapurna Interactive / Screen Burn.*

Townfall remains on track for **September 24, 2026**, set on the fictional Scottish island of **St. Amelia** as a standalone entry. Konami showed additional story and character material, with messages from Jovan Adepo, Terry O'Quinn and Kezia Burrows, and writer/director Jon McKellan discussed the story and characters directly. Konami also confirmed the Japanese voice cast for Simon, Zoe and Richard. As stated ahead of the broadcast, **there was no announcement regarding a Silent Hill remake** — that held true for the show itself.

### Castlevania: Belmont's Curse

Belmont's Curse stays on its **October 15, 2026** date. Konami released a new "Bloody Tears" trailer, named for the series' iconic track, showing additional story moments, characters and bosses centered on **Rose Belmont**'s fight against a returning Dracula.

### KONAMI Wai Wai World Craft

Konami also showed *KONAMI Wai Wai World Craft*, a social game-creation platform built around three pillars: Create, Play and Connect. Players can build, customize and share their own games, adjusting rules, enemies, mechanics and worlds, with characters drawn from Konami franchises including *Contra* and *Castlevania* available to use.

### eFootball: Kick-Off!

*eFootball: Kick-Off!*'s League Mode arrives on Nintendo Switch 2 through a free update on **September 24**, adding clubs from leagues across Europe, South America, Asia, Oceania and Japan's J.League.
