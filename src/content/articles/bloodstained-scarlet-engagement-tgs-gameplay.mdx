505 Games and developer ArtPlay used part of Xbox's Tokyo Game Show 2026 broadcast to show the most extensive look yet at *Bloodstained: The Scarlet Engagement*, the gothic action-RPG prequel to *Bloodstained: Ritual of the Night*. The publisher's own framing calls it the fullest gameplay footage released since the game's 2025 announcement, and it's the first time this specific two-character system has been shown in real combat rather than described in a press release.

<YouTube id="FCnw7JmIEHM" title="Bloodstained: The Scarlet Engagement Tokyo Game Show 2026 Trailer" />

## The Buddy System, Shown in Motion

The footage centers on protagonists **Leonard Brandon**, a young fighter aligned with the Church's Black Wolves, and **Alexander Kyteler**, a knight of the Kingdom's White Stags. 505 Games confirms this is the first mainline Bloodstained game to let players control two protagonists directly, through what the publisher calls a Buddy System: one character is player-controlled while the other assists in combat, and players can swap which one they're directly controlling in real time. The trailer shows both working together against traps and environmental obstacles as well as enemies, consistent with 505 Games' own description of the system rather than something inferred purely from the footage.

## Combat, Crafting and What's New Here

505 Games describes combat built around what it calls the Epiphany Combat System, through which players unlock additional moves and stronger attacks as they fight, alongside faster combat pacing against both smaller enemies and considerably larger ones. The trailer's footage backs that up visually — enemy encounters range from small skirmishes to fights against clearly oversized monsters, with Leonard and Alexander rapidly switching which one is under direct control mid-encounter — though the publisher hasn't detailed exactly how move unlocks are earned or how deep the system goes beyond that framing. Crafting and cooking systems are also confirmed as ways to strengthen the two protagonists, and armor customization visibly changes how characters look rather than existing as a stat-only system. None of that is new information specific to this footage, but seeing the Buddy System actually swap control mid-fight is: earlier material described the mechanic in the abstract, while this trailer is the first to show what that switch looks like in practice.

## A Cursed England, Rendered at a Larger Scale

The game is set in a cursed version of 16th-century England, before the events of *Ritual of the Night*, where a floating castle under the control of a figure called **Demon Lord Elias** has begun terrorizing the surrounding land with demonic forces. ArtPlay describes this as the largest map in the Bloodstained series to date, spanning both castle interiors and outdoor environments. The trailer also confirms a dynamic day-night cycle — dawn, day, dusk, and night — that changes how players interact with parts of the world, though 505 Games hasn't specified exactly which systems it affects beyond that general description.

## What Isn't Confirmed Yet

The footage introduces new environments and enemy types, but 505 Games hasn't published a specific enemy roster, a boss list, or details on how many hours of content the finished game will offer. Nothing in the trailer should be read as confirming a story synopsis beyond Elias's castle and the two protagonists' shared goal of stopping it, and the publisher hasn't detailed how directly players will be able to control both Leonard and Alexander simultaneously versus one at a time with an AI-assisted partner.

## Platforms and Release: Still 2027

*Bloodstained: The Scarlet Engagement* remains confirmed for PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, with a 2027 release window following an earlier delay from its original 2026 target. 505 Games hasn't announced a specific release date beyond that window.

## Franchise Context, Briefly

*The Scarlet Engagement* is directed by series creator Koji Igarashi at ArtPlay, the same studio behind *Ritual of the Night*. As a prequel, it doesn't require playing the earlier game first, though the connection gives series fans a sense of how this cursed corner of Bloodstained's world predates the story they already know. The game was first announced in June 2025 and originally targeted a 2026 release before shifting to the current 2027 window; 505 Games hasn't detailed a specific reason for the change.
