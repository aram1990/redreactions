*American Horror Story: 13* premieres **Thursday, September 24 at 9 p.m. ET on FX and Hulu** in the US, and **Friday, September 25 on Disney+** for international viewers. It's a **13-half-hour-episode season**, released as three episodes a week for the first two weeks, then two a week through the finale on October 29 (October 30 internationally, per Disney+'s schedule).

The season brings back the franchise's biggest names — Sarah Paulson, Evan Peters, Jessica Lange, Angela Bassett, Emma Roberts, Kathy Bates, Billie Lourd, Gabourey Sidibe, Leslie Grossman and more — and, notably, several of them are returning as **specific, previously established characters** rather than new roles. FX's September 10 full trailer resolved much of what was still unconfirmed when this guide first published, most notably that Jessica Lange isn't returning as one character — she's playing four. Here's what FX and Disney have actually confirmed, kept separate from what outlets are guessing.

## Confirmed: Which Characters Are Actually Returning

The September 10 full trailer goes considerably further than the August teaser and Disney's original seven-character press list. Several actors are confirmed playing **multiple** past characters, not one each:

- **Jessica Lange as four separate characters**: Constance Langdon (*Murder House*), Sister Jude (*Asylum*), Fiona Goode (*Coven*) and Elsa Mars (*Freak Show*). The trailer shows glimpses of all four; this is now the clearest "greatest hits" case in the season.
- **Evan Peters as three separate characters**: Rubber Man / Tate Langdon (*Murder House*), Kai Anderson (*Cult*), and — newly confirmed by this trailer — **James Patrick March** (*Hotel*). It's March who delivers the trailer's key line, "It's witch-hunting season," a piece of actual trailer dialogue rather than marketing copy layered over the footage.
- **Sarah Paulson as Cordelia Goode** (*Coven*)
- **Emma Roberts as Madison Montgomery** (*Coven*)
- **Angela Bassett as Marie Laveau** (*Coven*)
- **John Carroll Lynch as Twisty the Clown** (*Freak Show*)
- **Billie Lourd as Winter Anderson** (*Cult*) — newly confirmed by this trailer, resolving what was previously an educated guess

Beyond that, TVLine's coverage — sourced to FX's own first-look images — also puts **Mat Fraser back as Paul the Illustrated Seal**, **Gabourey Sidibe back as Queenie** and **Leslie Grossman back as Coco St. Pierre Vanderbilt**, all *Coven*/*Freak Show* roles matching actors already confirmed in the season 13 cast.

## Newly Confirmed by the Full Trailer

- **John Waters as Mr. Phibes**, a new character — a medical specialist working alongside Constance Langdon in the *Murder House* setting. This is a new role for the season, not a returning one, and resolves what was previously an unconfirmed-character cast credit.
- **Kathy Bates visibly appears in the trailer as Madame Delphine LaLaurie** (*Coven*). This is trailer-visible, not yet a named confirmation from FX or Disney's own press materials — a meaningfully stronger basis than the earlier "informed guess," but still one tier below an official character-list confirmation.

## Confirmed Cast, Still Unconfirmed Character

**Frances Conroy** and **Joey Pollari** remain confirmed in the cast without a stated returning character. **Mena Suvari** is confirmed in the cast; her likely return as Elizabeth Short (the Black Dahlia, from *Hotel*) is still reported but explicitly hedged as unconfirmed even by the outlets reporting it — this trailer didn't resolve that one.

## What Still Isn't Confirmed

- **Cody Fern as Michael Langdon.** Fan speculation has pointed to Instagram hints, but Fern is not in Disney's official cast list, and no FX confirmation exists. The September 10 trailer didn't change this.
- **Cara Delevingne's role**, if any — still linked to the season in press coverage without a pinned-down character or confirmed involvement.
- **Jamie Brewer's specific character.** Still confirmed in the cast; whether that means Nan, Adelaide or someone new still hasn't been said.
- **A full plot synopsis.** FX has described the season as centering on the number 13 and its superstitions, pulling threads from across the anthology's history — Evan Peters has called it a "greatest-hits" season — but no season-wide plot summary has been released.

## The Premise, As Far As It's Been Described

FX and Ryan Murphy have framed *American Horror Story: 13* as a return to Miss Robichaux's Academy and the *Coven* mythology specifically, expanded to pull in characters from other seasons rather than functioning as a direct, contained *Coven* sequel. Murphy has described it as something closer to an anthology-wide reunion than a single-season story — closer to "everyone in one house" than "one new haunting." Beyond that framing, FX has not published a plot synopsis, and nothing here should be read as confirming specific story events.

## The Bottom Line

The date is settled — September 24 in the US, September 25 everywhere Disney+ carries it, 13 half-hour episodes — and the full trailer has settled most of the cast question too: Jessica Lange as four characters, Evan Peters as three, Billie Lourd and Kathy Bates' likely roles resolved. What's still genuinely open is how many of those "greatest hits" characters get real screen time versus a cameo, and whether the number-13 framing is more than marketing language. That's a question the premiere itself will answer, not the trailer.
