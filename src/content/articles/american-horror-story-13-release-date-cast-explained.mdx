*American Horror Story: 13* premieres **Thursday, September 24 at 9 p.m. ET on FX and Hulu** in the US, and **Friday, September 25 on Disney+** for international viewers. That's the full 13-episode season, released as three episodes a week for the first two weeks, then two a week through the finale on October 29 (October 30 internationally, per Disney+'s schedule).

The season brings back the franchise's biggest names — Sarah Paulson, Evan Peters, Jessica Lange, Angela Bassett, Emma Roberts, Kathy Bates, Billie Lourd, Gabourey Sidibe, Leslie Grossman and more — and, notably, several of them are returning as **specific, previously established characters** rather than new roles. Here's what FX and Disney have actually confirmed, kept separate from what outlets are guessing.

## Confirmed: Which Characters Are Actually Returning

Disney's own press materials name seven returning characters directly: **Constance Langdon, Cordelia Goode, Madison Montgomery, Marie Laveau, Rubber Man, Twisty the Clown and Kai Anderson**. Matched against the season's official cast list and each character's exclusive casting history in the franchise, that gives a clean, confirmed picture:

- **Sarah Paulson as Cordelia Goode** (*Coven*)
- **Jessica Lange as Constance Langdon** (*Murder House*)
- **Emma Roberts as Madison Montgomery** (*Coven*)
- **Angela Bassett as Marie Laveau** (*Coven*)
- **Evan Peters as Rubber Man / Tate Langdon and Kai Anderson** (*Murder House* and *Cult*) — Disney's release names both Rubber Man and Kai Anderson among the returning characters, and Peters is the only actor either role has ever been cast with
- **John Carroll Lynch as Twisty the Clown** (*Freak Show*)

Beyond that named list, TVLine's coverage — sourced to FX's own first-look images — also puts **Mat Fraser back as Paul the Illustrated Seal**, **Gabourey Sidibe back as Queenie** and **Leslie Grossman back as Coco St. Pierre Vanderbilt**, all *Coven*/*Freak Show* roles matching actors already confirmed in the season 13 cast.

## Confirmed Cast, Unconfirmed Character

Several major names are officially confirmed as part of the cast but without a stated returning character: **Frances Conroy, Kathy Bates, Billie Lourd, John Waters** and **Joey Pollari**. Kathy Bates returning likely means Madame Delphine LaLaurie given her *Coven* history, and Billie Lourd has played both Winter and Mallory Prescott in past seasons, but neither pairing has been officially confirmed by FX — treat those as informed guesses, not settled fact.

**Mena Suvari** is confirmed in the cast; her likely return as Elizabeth Short (the Black Dahlia, from *Hotel*) is reported but explicitly hedged as unconfirmed even by the outlets reporting it.

## What Isn't Confirmed

- **Cody Fern as Michael Langdon.** Fan speculation has pointed to Instagram hints, but Fern is not in Disney's official cast list, and no FX confirmation exists.
- **Cara Delevingne's role**, if any — she's been linked to the season in press coverage but her character, and in some reports her involvement at all, hasn't been pinned down officially.
- **Jamie Brewer's specific character.** She's confirmed in the cast; whether that means Nan, Adelaide or someone new hasn't been said.
- **A full plot synopsis.** FX has described the season as centering on the number 13 and its superstitions, pulling threads from across the anthology's history — Evan Peters has called it a "greatest-hits" season — but no season-wide plot summary has been released.

## The Premise, As Far As It's Been Described

FX and Ryan Murphy have framed *American Horror Story: 13* as a return to Miss Robichaux's Academy and the *Coven* mythology specifically, expanded to pull in characters from other seasons rather than functioning as a direct, contained *Coven* sequel. Murphy has described it as something closer to an anthology-wide reunion than a single-season story — closer to "everyone in one house" than "one new haunting." Beyond that framing, FX has not published a plot synopsis, and nothing here should be read as confirming specific story events.

## The Bottom Line

The date is settled — September 24 in the US, September 25 everywhere Disney+ carries it — and so is the headline cast. What's still genuinely open is how many of those "greatest hits" characters get real screen time versus a cameo, and whether the number-13 framing is more than marketing language. That's a question the premiere itself will answer, not the teaser.
