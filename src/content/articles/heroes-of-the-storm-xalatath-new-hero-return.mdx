Blizzard used its BlizzCon 2026 Opening Ceremony to announce something *Heroes of the Storm* hasn't gotten since 2020: a brand-new hero. Xal'atath, the Void-wielding antagonist from *World of Warcraft*, is joining the Nexus roster — the game's first new character since Hogger arrived six years ago.

## What's Confirmed

- **Xal'atath is officially coming to Heroes of the Storm**, announced by Blizzard President Johanna Faries during the BlizzCon 2026 Opening Ceremony
- **PTR begins Monday, September 14, 2026**
- **Full/live release is September 28, 2026**
- Role: **Ranged Assassin**, built around her Void powers from *World of Warcraft*
- Confirmed ability kit: Trait **Void Volley**, Q **Shadow Mark**, W **Void Step**, E **Dark Heart Ritual**, with Heroics **Void Eruption** and **Void Convergence**
- This is officially the **first new hero added to Heroes of the Storm since Hogger in 2020**
- Faries said Blizzard "might even have another hero on the way in the near future" — a tease, not a confirmed second hero, name, or date

![In-game screenshot of Xal'atath's Heroes of the Storm model, shown during the BlizzCon 2026 Opening Ceremony reveal.](/images/articles/heroes-of-the-storm-xalatath-new-hero-return/xalatath-blizzcon-2026-reveal-screenshot.webp "Blizzard Entertainment")

## Why Xal'atath Matters Here

Heroes of the Storm entered what Blizzard itself described in 2022 as reduced, maintenance-level support — balance patches and bug fixes, without new heroes, new battlegrounds, or the game's former release cadence. Hogger, added in 2020, has stood as the game's last new character for six years since. A brand-new hero reveal, backed by an actual PTR date and a full release date less than three weeks out, is a genuine, confirmed break from that pattern — not a rumor or a wishlist item.

Xal'atath herself is also a significant pick thematically: she's been the primary antagonist across multiple recent *World of Warcraft* expansions, from *Legion* through the current *Midnight* expansion, making her one of the highest-profile Warcraft villains Blizzard could have chosen to headline this return.

## What This Doesn't Confirm

It's worth being precise about the boundaries of this announcement, because it's easy to read a new hero as a bigger signal than what Blizzard actually said. Blizzard has **not** announced a full "3.0" relaunch or a return to Heroes of the Storm's earlier active-development cadence. There's no confirmed return of HGC esports, no announced battleground roadmap beyond this hero, and no commitment to regular future hero releases on a set schedule. Faries' comment about "another hero on the way" is a tease of future possibility, not a named hero, a date, or a formal roadmap. Treat Xal'atath as a real, dated addition to the game — and treat everything beyond that as still unconfirmed.

## PTR vs. Live: What Changes When

The PTR period, starting September 14, gives players roughly two weeks to test Xal'atath's kit and see balance adjustments before the September 28 full release, when she becomes generally available (with pre-purchase reportedly available following the BlizzCon announcement itself). That's a standard Heroes of the Storm testing window for a new hero, consistent with how the game handled hero launches during its more active development years.

## Bottom Line

Xal'atath is a real, dated, confirmed addition to Heroes of the Storm — PTR September 14, live September 28, arriving as a ranged assassin built around Void abilities — and she's officially the game's first new hero since Hogger in 2020. What she isn't is proof that Heroes of the Storm is returning to full live-service development, esports, or a regular hero-release schedule. Blizzard has left the door open to more, without walking through it yet.
