*Reacher* Season 4 is officially underway on Prime Video, with Alan Ritchson back as Jack Reacher and the franchise already preparing its next handoff.

The first three episodes arrived on August 12, with new episodes now releasing weekly through the **September 16** finale. Season 4 adapts Lee Child's *Gone Tomorrow*, sending Reacher into a new conspiracy after a subway encounter with a distressed stranger goes horribly wrong.

<YouTube id="PCbp1FfipPg" title="REACHER Season 4 - Official Trailer | Prime Video" />

Amazon's latest published worldwide Prime Original ranking had *Reacher* at **No. 4 for August 3–9** — before Season 4 premiered. That chart counts accounts that watched any available season, so it shows continued interest in the franchise rather than confirmed Season 4 premiere performance. Amazon has not yet published an official Season 4 opening viewership total.

## Neagley Arrives With the Finale

September 16 will also launch *Neagley*, Prime Video's first *Reacher* spin-off.

Maria Sten returns as Frances Neagley, now working as a private investigator in Chicago. After learning that a close friend from her past died in a suspicious accident, she sets out to uncover what happened.

<img src="/images/articles/reacher-season-4-neagley-spin-off/reacher-season-4-neagley-spin-off-neagley.jpg" alt="Maria Sten as Frances Neagley holding a teddy bear in Prime Video's Neagley." loading="lazy" />

Prime Video says **all eight episodes will release at once**, immediately following the *Reacher* Season 4 finale. Alan Ritchson will also appear as Jack Reacher in a guest role rather than sharing the lead.

## Reacher Is Already Renewed

The franchise is not stopping there. Prime Video renewed *Reacher* for Season 5 in May, with the next season set to adapt Lee Child's *Make Me*. Amanda Ip, Jay Baruchel, Ciara Bravo and Kevin Durand are among the new cast members already announced.

Prime Video has not announced a Season 5 premiere date.
