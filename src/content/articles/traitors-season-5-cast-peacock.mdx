Peacock has confirmed the full celebrity cast for *The Traitors* Season 5, per Deadline. The season is set to launch in the new year, though no exact premiere date has been announced.

## The confirmed cast

Per Deadline's reporting, the Season 5 lineup includes: Kristin Chenoweth, *The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives*' Whitney Leavitt, RuPaul's Drag Race star Bianca Del Rio, creator Boman Martinez-Reid, *The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City*'s Bronwyn Newport, *Happy Endings*' Casey Wilson, Olympic snowboarder Chloe Kim, Australian pop star Cody Simpson, *Southern Charm*'s Craig Conover, *The Real Housewives of Atlanta*'s Cynthia Bailey, *Lost*'s Dominic Monaghan, *Next Gen NYC*'s Emira D'Spain, *The Real Housewives of Potomac*'s Gizelle Bryant, *Love Island* narrator Iain Stirling, *Big Brother*'s Jag Bains, *Love Island USA*'s JaNa Craig, *The Bachelor*'s Joey Graziadei, *Queer As Folk*'s Johnny Sibilly, *Survivor*'s Michele Fitzgerald and Q Burdette, *The Challenge*'s Rachel Robinson, former NFL player Rashad Jennings, and *The Amazing Race*'s Taylor Hale.

## Why Peacock is leaning into this now

The cast reveal comes during what Deadline describes as a busy week for the Studio Lambert-produced format. *The Traitors* was renewed for three more seasons — taking the show through Season 8 — alongside a new deal for host Alan Cumming. It also won its third consecutive Emmy for Outstanding Reality Competition Program, with Cumming winning his third consecutive Emmy for Outstanding Host. NBC additionally launched *The Traitors: New Blood*, a civilian version of the format, the night before the cast news broke.

## How the game actually works

*The Traitors* is based on the Dutch format *De Verraders*. Cumming selects a subset of players to be Traitors at the season's start; the Traitors secretly eliminate Faithful players one by one while trying to stay undetected, and the Faithful attempt to identify and vote out the Traitors at roundtable gatherings. If any Traitors remain at the end, they split the prize money among themselves; if none remain, the Faithful split it instead. The series is executive produced by Cumming, Stephen Lambert, Mike Cotton, Sam Rees-Jones, Rosie Franks, Tim Harcourt, Jack Burgess and Darrell Olsen.

## What's still unknown

Peacock hasn't set an exact premiere date beyond "the new year," and character/role assignments — who's a Traitor and who's Faithful — are determined during filming and aren't something a cast announcement would reveal. Treat any premiere-date guesses beyond "early 2027" as speculation until Peacock confirms a date.
