## What's actually new from today's reveal

PSYREN's production committee released a new key visual and main trailer on September 11, 2026, alongside confirmation of additional cast members: **Yūko Kaida** as Matsuri Yagumo and **Nobuyuki Hiyama** as Dolkey, joining previously announced leads **Rikuya Yasuda** as Ageha Yoshina and **Mayuko Kazama** as Sakurako Amamiya. The opening theme, "WARNING!!" by CLAN QUEEN, was also confirmed today. None of this changes what was already known — the October 5 date and full-story adaptation plan were confirmed earlier this year — but it's the first time fans have seen actual animation and heard the theme song.

## A long-delayed adaptation, adapting the whole manga

PSYREN, written and illustrated by Toshiaki Iwashiro, ran in *Weekly Shōnen Jump* from 2008 to 2010 without ever getting an anime — until now, roughly 16 years after it began. Satelight's production is confirmed to be planned as a full adaptation covering the manga's entire story rather than a partial or open-ended run, following the series' premise: a red "PSYREN" phone card pulls Ageha and classmate Sakurako into a devastated future version of their city.

## Staff and returning talent

Satelight's own credits page lists **Katsumi Ono** directing, **Shin Yoshida** on series composition, and **Haku Okuma** handling character design. Beyond Kaida and Hiyama's newly announced roles, the returning core cast anchors the show around Yasuda and Kazama as the two leads.

## A real-world tie-in event

The production committee is also running a promotional collaboration with NTT East, setting up "special public telephone booths" tied to the series in Shibuya on October 11 — a nod to the story's own red PSYREN phone cards, which is what actually pulls Ageha into the plot in the first place. It's a one-off event rather than an ongoing tie-in, but it's the kind of detail that signals how much promotional weight is behind this premiere after such a long wait.

## What's still unconfirmed

Neither Satelight nor the production committee has announced an exact episode count or cour count for the season. An English dub, a Netflix or Hulu release, or any worldwide day-and-date simulcast beyond Crunchyroll's confirmed international streaming haven't been announced either — treat those as unknown until stated directly by the production committee or a streaming partner.
