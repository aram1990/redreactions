You don't need to have seen all 12 previous seasons of *American Horror Story* before *13* premieres September 24. But Ryan Murphy has been clear that this isn't a contained new story — it pulls characters from across the anthology's history — so a little targeted homework goes further here than it would for most seasons. This is a practical route through what actually helps, not a full rewatch assignment.

*This guide contains some spoilers for the endings of Coven, Apocalypse and Murder House, kept as light as possible and limited to what's needed to explain how the seasons connect. For everything currently confirmed about Season 13 itself — cast, premiere date, teaser — see our [full AHS: 13 guide](/articles/american-horror-story-13-release-date-cast-explained/).*

## The Quick Answer

| Season | Priority | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Coven* (Season 3) | **Essential** | Home base for most confirmed returning characters and the Supreme mythology |
| *Apocalypse* (Season 8) | **Highly recommended** | Shows how AHS seasons actually connect, not just share actors |
| *Murder House* (Season 1) | **Recommended** | Deepens Apocalypse and explains Constance and Rubber Man |
| *Freak Show*, *Cult* | **Character-specific only** | Only needed if you want background on Twisty, Paul, or Kai Anderson specifically |
| Everything else | **Optional / completionist** | No confirmed Season 13 tie-in currently |

## Why This Isn't Just a Coven Rewatch

Ryan Murphy has pushed back directly on the assumption that *13* is a *Coven* sequel. "Everybody thinks it's a Coven season and it's not," he said in July. "It's all the seasons. It's like the Avengers! They all come together to defeat the ultimate evil." He's also said Sarah Paulson could play six or seven roles across the season and that Jessica Lange is playing multiple past characters.

Treat that as the promotional framing it is, not a character list. FX and Disney's actual press materials confirm far fewer specifics: seven named returning characters, tied to particular actors. Lange, for instance, is confirmed back as *Murder House*'s Constance Langdon — not, as of now, as *Coven*'s Fiona Goode, *Asylum*'s Sister Jude, or *Freak Show*'s Elsa Mars, whatever Murphy's "all four" comment might suggest. Murphy's comments describe the season's ambition; FX's cast confirmations describe what's actually locked. This guide follows the confirmations.

## Essential: Coven (Season 3)

*Coven* is where most of *13*'s confirmed cast actually comes from. It follows Miss Robichaux's Academy, a school for young witches in New Orleans, and the fight to determine the next **Supreme** — the most powerful witch of a generation — after the reigning Supreme's decline.

Three of the seven characters FX has directly confirmed for *13* originate here: **Cordelia Goode** (Sarah Paulson), the academy's headmistress; **Madison Montgomery** (Emma Roberts), a self-absorbed young witch with formidable power; and **Marie Laveau** (Angela Bassett), the immortal Voodoo Queen of New Orleans and the coven's historic rival. **Queenie** (Gabourey Sidibe), whose actor is also confirmed to return, is a *Coven* original as well. You don't need every subplot — just enough to know who these people are and what Miss Robichaux's Academy is, which is close to unavoidable if you watch even a few episodes.

## Highly Recommended: Apocalypse (Season 8)

*Apocalypse* matters less for its own plot and more for what it proves: that AHS crossovers aren't just actors playing new roles — the same characters can genuinely return and interact across seasons.

Structurally, *Apocalypse* is built as a *Murder House*/*Coven* crossover. Its central antagonist, Michael Langdon, is the adult son of *Murder House*'s Vivien Harmon and Tate Langdon, and much of the season's second half is the *Coven* witches — including Cordelia, Madison and Queenie — confronting him directly. It's a useful preview of exactly the kind of cross-season collision Murphy has promised for *13*, even though Michael Langdon himself isn't among FX's confirmed *13* characters.

One character note worth having going in: **Coco St. Pierre Vanderbilt** (Leslie Grossman), also confirmed for *13*, is an *Apocalypse* original rather than a *Coven* one — she's introduced there as a shallow socialite before being revealed as a witch partway through the season.

## Recommended: Murder House (Season 1)

The franchise's first season is worth watching mainly because it explains two confirmed *13* characters directly: **Constance Langdon** (Jessica Lange), the venomous next-door neighbor with her own dark history, and **Rubber Man**, whose *Murder House* identity is Tate Langdon (Evan Peters) — the same actor also confirmed to return as *Cult*'s Kai Anderson.

You can follow *Apocalypse* without having seen *Murder House* first, since it reintroduces the necessary background. But watching *Murder House* beforehand makes Constance and Tate land with their original weight rather than as characters you're meeting mid-explanation.

## Character-Specific: Freak Show and Cult

Two more seasons are worth knowing about only if you care about a specific confirmed character, not because they connect to the main throughline:

- ***Freak Show*** (Season 4) introduces **Twisty the Clown** (John Carroll Lynch) and **Paul the Illustrated Seal** (Mat Fraser), both confirmed to return for *13*. Neither character has any confirmed tie to *Coven* or *Murder House*'s events.
- ***Cult*** (Season 7) is where Evan Peters' **Kai Anderson** originates — a separate role from his *Rubber Man*/Tate Langdon casting. Watching it tells you who Kai is; it doesn't connect to the Coven/Apocalypse/Murder House throughline.

Skip either if you're short on time. They add character background, not plot continuity.

## Practical Watch Routes

These are editorial suggestions, not an official FX viewing order — FX hasn't published one for *13*.

**Fastest route:** *Coven* → *Apocalypse*

**Best continuity route:** *Murder House* → *Coven* → *Apocalypse*

**Full franchise route:** all 12 prior seasons, for completionists only. Nothing currently confirmed about *13* requires it — Murphy's "all the seasons" framing describes ambition and possibility, not a confirmed guarantee that every installment pays off equally.

## What's Still Unconfirmed

Several actors are locked for *13* without a confirmed character to go with them: Frances Conroy, Kathy Bates, Billie Lourd, John Waters and Joey Pollari. Kathy Bates as *Coven*'s Madame LaLaurie and Billie Lourd as either Winter or Mallory are reasonable guesses given their history with the franchise, but neither is official. If those or other characters get confirmed before premiere, the relevant season becomes more worth prioritizing — check our [AHS: 13 cast guide](/articles/american-horror-story-13-release-date-cast-explained/) for the latest confirmed list.

## The Bottom Line

If you watch nothing else, *Coven* covers the most confirmed ground. Add *Apocalypse* if you want to understand how these crossovers actually work rather than just which actors are back, and add *Murder House* if you want Constance and Tate to land properly. Everything past that is for people who want the full picture, not people trying to understand *13*.
