Com2uS closed out part of Xbox's Tokyo Game Show 2026 broadcast with the first gameplay trailer for *GACHIAKUTA: BREAKOUT*, its console and PC adaptation of Kei Urana's manga and its TV anime. The game was previously known by the working title *Gachiakuta: The Game*; September 17 is when it got both its official name and its first real look at how it plays, rather than just how it looks — including, for the first time, specifics on its four-character roster, co-op support, and base-building system.

## What the Trailer Actually Shows

The footage centers on protagonist Rudo fighting through junk-strewn, industrial environments using melee combat built around his ability to pull power out of discarded objects — visually rendered as glowing red energy wrapped around his hands and weapons, and, in other shots, channeled into improvised close-combat weapons assembled from scrap. A major sequence pits Zanka against Jabber Wonger, a sadistic member of the Raiders in the source manga and anime; that fight is the trailer's clearest boss-style encounter, with Jabber functioning as an enemy the Cleaners fight rather than a playable character himself. Enemies also include the Trash Beasts that populate the Pit — hostile creatures formed out of discarded objects rather than conventional monster designs. The visual style keeps the anime's thick outlines, high-contrast lighting, and cel-shaded character models rather than pushing toward a more conventional realistic look, and the environments carry that same junk-world identity through into three dimensions rather than flattening it into generic rubble.

## Four Playable Cleaners, Each With a Distinct Vital Instrument

Com2uS confirms four playable Cleaners at this stage: Rudo, Enjin, Zanka, and Riyo, each fighting with a personal Vital Instrument — the game's term for the Anima-imbued objects Cleaners wield in combat. Rudo fights with a pair of gloves, transforming discarded garbage into weapons on the fly rather than relying on a fixed moveset. Enjin uses a heavy-hitting umbrella built around crowd control and large special attacks. Zanka fights with a staff, chaining together rapid, successive strikes rather than single heavy blows. Riyo wields oversized scissors, built for precision cuts against the Pit's mutated Trash Beasts. That's a real four-character roster confirmed for this stage of development — not a guess based on who appears in the trailer, since Com2uS's own material names all four directly as playable Cleaners.

## Cleaners HQ, Co-op and Graffiti Collection

Beyond combat, Com2uS confirms a base-building layer: Cleaners HQ can be customized and expanded using materials scavenged from missions in the Pit, giving runs through the wasteland a purpose beyond the immediate fight. Real-time co-op is confirmed, letting players tackle the Pit's missions together rather than solo only. Com2uS also confirms a graffiti-collection system, with hidden graffiti scattered throughout the Pit for players to find. None of these three systems are inferred from footage alone — they come from Com2uS's own confirmed feature list for the game.

## What Com2uS Confirms About the Game Itself

Com2uS describes *GACHIAKUTA: BREAKOUT* as a survival action RPG built around fast, melee-focused combat, set in the world of the Pit — the trash-filled abyss the anime's cast is cast into. The developer's own framing emphasizes "premium cartoon-style rendering and evocative visual design" meant to carry the source material's specific look into a fully playable game, rather than a generic anime-tie-in art style. Beyond the confirmed systems above, Com2uS hasn't published a detailed mechanical breakdown — a stamina meter, skill trees, weapon crafting depth, or mission structure beyond "scavenge, fight, return to HQ" haven't been laid out in official material yet, so treat anything beyond what's specifically confirmed as visible in footage rather than independently established.

## Platforms and Release: Not an Xbox Exclusive

Because this reveal happened during an Xbox broadcast, it would be easy to assume *GACHIAKUTA: BREAKOUT* is Xbox-only — it isn't. Com2uS's own confirmed platform lineup is PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam, with Xbox Play Anywhere support specifically for the Xbox and Windows versions. The Xbox showcase setting explains why Xbox Wire's own recap leans on Xbox-specific language like Play Anywhere, but that's a feature of the Xbox version, not evidence the game skips other platforms. A specific release date hasn't been announced; Com2uS has only confirmed a 2027 launch window.

## From Manga Panel to Playable World

*Gachiakuta* began as Kei Urana's manga before becoming a TV anime, and *BREAKOUT* is Com2uS's attempt to carry that same story world into a game rather than build a loosely licensed spinoff. Development was first confirmed publicly in December 2025, with an initial teaser released the same day as the anime's second-season finale aired in Japan — a deliberate pairing that signaled the game as a continuation of the anime's momentum rather than a separate side project. The reveal doesn't clarify how closely the game's story tracks the anime's plot, whether it's a retelling of existing chapters or an original scenario set in the same world, or how far into the source material's cast and locations the finished game will go.

## What's Still Unknown

Com2uS hasn't detailed a story synopsis distinct from the source anime, whether the four confirmed Cleaners represent the entire launch roster or just what's playable at this stage, exactly how deep Cleaners HQ customization goes, or an exact release date beyond the 2027 window. Nothing in the gameplay trailer or its surrounding coverage should be read as confirming a demo, pricing, matchmaking specifics for co-op, or how directly the game's story follows the anime's own chapter-by-chapter events.
