World of Warcraft: The Last Titan was announced back in 2023 alongside the rest of the Worldsoul Saga, but it's had almost no new material since. BlizzCon 2026 changed that with **"COLOSSAL,"** a new teaser that returns to Northrend and gives the expansion its first real visual identity — while still leaving most of the actual game unrevealed.

## CONFIRMED BY BLIZZARD

- The Last Titan remains the **final chapter of the Worldsoul Saga**, the trilogy that began with The War Within (2024) and continued through Midnight (2026)
- Senior Game Director **Ion Hazzikostas** described it as **"the culmination of over two decades of World of Warcraft"** — while also cautioning it will be "laying the groundwork for what comes next," not wrapping up every storyline
- Blizzard's own materials call it **"more than a return to Northrend"**
- The Last Titan directly follows **Midnight: Eclipse** (patch 12.2), which centers on confronting Xal'atath at the Worldcore
- Blizzard says fuller details arrive **"early next year, after the events of Midnight: Eclipse"** — consistent with the widely reported **2027** release window, though no specific date has been given
- The colossal figure shown in the teaser is **not identified** by Blizzard

## What "COLOSSAL" Visibly Shows

The teaser is brief and mostly atmospheric: a blizzard-swept Northrend landscape, the **Wyrmrest Temple** visible in the snow, and an enormous, dark, Titan-like figure approaching it. The figure stomps toward the temple and fires a massive energy beam. That's the extent of what's actually shown on screen — no characters are shown speaking, no playable content is depicted, and no zones beyond this one snowy location are visible.

It's worth being precise about what this footage does and doesn't establish. The figure's scale and the temple's presence strongly evoke Northrend's established geography, but nothing here confirms a specific storyline beyond "something enormous is approaching Wyrmrest Temple." Treat the cinematic as a mood-setting reveal, not a gameplay or plot preview.

## The Colossal Figure: Confirmed vs. Unknown

Confirmed: it's large, it's Titan-like in design, and it targets Wyrmrest Temple with an offensive attack in the footage. Unconfirmed: its identity. Blizzard has not named the figure as Aman'Thul, Sargeras, Azeroth's own Titan-soul, or any other specific character from Warcraft lore. Any claim identifying it specifically is fan interpretation, not confirmed information — this piece isn't going to guess, because Blizzard hasn't said.

## Why Northrend, Specifically

Blizzard's own phrase — "more than a return to Northrend" — is a deliberate signal that this isn't simply nostalgia for a fan-favorite zone. The studio hasn't detailed which specific Northrend locations beyond Wyrmrest Temple will be involved, whether Icecrown Citadel or other classic sites factor in, or how the region will differ from its Wrath of the Lich King-era version. What's confirmed is the setting and the temple's visibility in the teaser itself — everything else about the region's role is still unannounced.

## How Midnight: Eclipse Bridges Into It

This is the part that's genuinely new information versus the 2023 reveal. Eclipse, patch 12.2 and the closing chapter of the current Midnight expansion, puts players face-to-face with Xal'atath at the Worldcore as she moves toward what Blizzard calls her "ultimate objective." Blizzard has described Eclipse's ending as setting up a "revelation" that leads directly into The Last Titan — meaning the two are explicitly connected narrative beats, not just sequential expansions. Blizzard has not detailed how Eclipse actually concludes or what that revelation specifically is, so treat the handoff as confirmed to exist without treating any guess at Eclipse's ending as fact.

## Release Window and What's Still Unknown

Blizzard's own wording points to early 2027, tied explicitly to Eclipse's conclusion, and widely corroborated as a "2027" target across coverage of the reveal — though no specific date, month, alpha, or beta timing has been announced. Beyond that: no zones beyond the Wyrmrest Temple area have been confirmed, no bosses, raids, or systems have been detailed, and the colossal figure's identity, motivations, and role in the actual expansion remain unstated. Blizzard has explicitly said more will come after Eclipse concludes, not before.

## Bottom Line

The real news here isn't "The Last Titan exists" — that's been known since 2023. It's that Blizzard has now shown its first real imagery (a Northrend blizzard, Wyrmrest Temple, an unnamed colossal figure attacking it), confirmed it as the Worldsoul Saga's finale and Hazzikostas's "culmination of over two decades," and explicitly tied its reveal timing to Midnight: Eclipse's own ending. Everything about the actual expansion — its zones, systems, and the giant figure's identity — is still ahead of us.
