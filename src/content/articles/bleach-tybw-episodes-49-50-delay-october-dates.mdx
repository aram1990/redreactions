**BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War — The Calamity has officially delayed its final two episodes.** Episode 49 and episode 50, originally due to close out the series this September, have been pushed to October 19 and October 26, 2026. This is a schedule change, not a cancellation — here's exactly what's moved, why, and what's airing in the meantime.

## What's Confirmed

- **Episode 49** now airs on the **TV Tokyo network October 19, 2026 at 24:00 JST**, on **BS TV Tokyo October 20 at 24:30 JST**, with Japanese streaming from **October 20 at noon JST**.
- **Episode 50** now airs on the **TV Tokyo network October 26, 2026 at 24:00 JST**, on **BS TV Tokyo October 27 at 24:30 JST**, with Japanese streaming from **October 27 at noon JST**.
- The production committee's stated reason is to allow **additional time to improve quality heading into the climax and deliver the remaining episodes in the best possible form** — not a production crisis, censorship issue, staff walkout, or any other unconfirmed cause.
- Broadcast times for **other Japanese networks and affiliate stations** will be announced separately by those stations.
- In the gap, TV Tokyo is airing a **"Climax Selection"** block of previously aired episodes rather than new content.

## A Quick Note on "24:00 JST"

Japanese TV schedules commonly list late-night programming using times past midnight — "24:00" on a given date means **12:00 AM the following calendar day**, not a fifth item at midnight on that date itself. So episode 49's "TV Tokyo, October 19 at 24:00 JST" slot airs in the very early hours of **October 20** by a standard 24-hour clock, even though the network lists it under the 19th. This is standard Japanese broadcast convention, not a separate change to the date itself.

## Old Schedule vs. New Schedule

| | Original Plan | New Date |
|---|---|---|
| Episode 49 | Mid-September 2026 | TV Tokyo Oct. 19 (24:00 JST) / BS TV Tokyo Oct. 20 (24:30 JST) / streaming Oct. 20 (noon JST) |
| Episode 50 | Late September 2026 | TV Tokyo Oct. 26 (24:00 JST) / BS TV Tokyo Oct. 27 (24:30 JST) / streaming Oct. 27 (noon JST) |

## Delay, Not Cancellation

To be clear: nothing about this announcement suggests BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War is being cut short, cancelled, or reworked. Both episodes are confirmed to still be airing — they've simply been moved back roughly a month, with the production committee framing it specifically as extra time to polish the final stretch of the story rather than any kind of production emergency. There's no confirmed information pointing to staff departures, censorship, or behind-the-scenes conflict, and none of that should be assumed from a schedule change alone.

## The Climax Selection Replacement Schedule

While episode 49 is on hold, TV Tokyo is filling its normal broadcast slot with a run of previously aired episodes under the "Climax Selection" banner:

| Date | Episode | Title |
|---|---|---|
| September 19 | Episode 7 | "BORN IN THE DARK" |
| September 26 | Episode 28 | "KILL THE KING" |
| October 5 | Episode 46 | "THE END" |
| October 12 | Episode 48 | "THE END TWO WORLD" |

These are reruns selected to lead back into the story ahead of the new episode 49 date, not new content.

## International Streaming

BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War has been streaming internationally on Hulu in the U.S. and Disney+ in other territories. The sources confirming this delay cover the Japanese broadcast and Japanese streaming schedule specifically; exact international release dates and times for episodes 49 and 50 on Hulu and Disney+ have not been separately confirmed at the time of writing, and shouldn't be assumed to match the JST times above without official confirmation from those platforms.

## What's Still Unknown

The official announcements don't specify a more detailed breakdown of what "quality improvements" involve, and broadcast times for Japanese networks beyond TV Tokyo and BS TV Tokyo hadn't been published as of this delay announcement. International platforms haven't issued their own separate schedule update as of publication.
