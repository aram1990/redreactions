*Daemons of the Shadow Realm* is getting a second season. The announcement landed almost immediately after the first season's 24th and final episode aired in Japan on September 19, 2026, alongside a new teaser video and key visual.

## What's Actually Confirmed

Official Japanese reporting states plainly: "TVアニメ「黄泉のツガイ」第2期の制作が決定" — "Season 2 production for the TV anime *Yomi no Tsugai* has been decided." That's a genuinely new season announcement, made after the first season wrapped, not a continuation of the first season's existing episode order.

**This is a different thing from the first season's second cour.** *Daemons of the Shadow Realm*'s first season already aired across two consecutive cours — the first from April 4 to June 20, 2026, and the second from July 4 to September 19, 2026 — covering a combined 24 episodes as one continuous season. That second-cour split happened months ago and is now finished. The newly announced Season 2 is a separate, additional season beyond those 24 episodes, confirmed only now that the first season has concluded.

## What the New Teaser Shows

The new teaser video features returning main characters **Yuru**, **Asa**, **Sayo-sama** and **Gabu-chan**, alongside new characters described as key to the story going forward. It also shows **Goryo**, identified as the leader of the Nishinomura faction pursuing Yuru and Asa. A "super teaser visual" was released alongside the footage.

At the time of writing, the specific teaser hasn't been independently verified against a citable official upload with a confirmed video ID, so it isn't embedded here — treat the character and footage details above as descriptive of what's been reported, not as a substitute for watching the official material once it's more widely available. As with any teaser, character appearances confirm their presence in upcoming material, not specific plot outcomes, alliances or fates.

## What's Still Not Announced

No premiere date or window has been set for Season 2. Official material hasn't confirmed which cast and staff members are specifically returning — while the same production team (director Masahiro Ando, Studio Bones Film) is reasonably expected to continue given the project's continuity, treat that as an assumption rather than a confirmed fact until officially stated for this new season specifically. Don't assume the manga's current pace or unresolved plotlines dictate a specific episode count or adaptation scope for Season 2 either.

## Quick Context: What Is Daemons of the Shadow Realm?

*Daemons of the Shadow Realm* (*Yomi no Tsugai* / 黄泉のツガイ) is based on the manga by **Hiromu Arakawa**, creator of *Fullmetal Alchemist*, serialized in Square Enix's *Monthly Shonen Gangan* since December 2021. The story follows twins Yuru and Asa, born in a remote Japanese mountain village and caught up in a supernatural conflict tied to their birthplace. The anime is produced by Studio Bones Film and directed by Masahiro Ando, and streams on Crunchyroll.
