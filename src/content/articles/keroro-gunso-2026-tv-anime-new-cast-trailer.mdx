Bandai Namco Pictures has confirmed the first full trailer for *Keroro Gunso☆*, a new TV anime bringing Sgt. Frog back to Japanese television for the first time since the original series ended in March 2011. It premieres October 3, and the entire Keroro Platoon has a new voice cast.

## When the New Sgt. Frog Anime Premieres

*Keroro Gunso☆* begins broadcasting Saturday, October 3, at 9:30 a.m. JST on TV Tokyo and its five affiliate stations — six networks total. That's the detail worth sitting with: this is the franchise's first genuinely new television series in over fifteen years, not counting the 24-episode *Keroro* series that aired in 2014.

## What the First Trailer Actually Shows

The roughly 90-second PV introduces the new cast in character, voicing lines as Keroro and the platoon move through familiar poses and settings — Keroro striking his usual dramatic stance, the Hinata household, and glimpses of Fuyuki and Natsumi. It's built to showcase the new voices rather than reveal plot: BN Pictures hasn't described any specific story beats beyond the premise itself, and nothing in the footage should be read as confirming particular episodes or arcs.

## The Entire Keroro Platoon Has a New TV Cast

Every platoon member has a new voice actor for this series: Makoto Koichi as Keroro, Rika Nagae as Tamama, Toshiki Masuda as Giroro, Kosuke Echigoya as Kururu, and Keishi Inomata as Dororo. This is a complete cast change specifically for the new TV anime — not a franchise-wide replacement. The 20th-anniversary theatrical film that opened June 26, 2026 used the franchise's original, longtime cast, including Kumiko Watanabe as Keroro, who has voiced the character since 2004. The film and the new TV series are separate projects made with separate voice casts.

## The Classic Premise Is Back

BN Pictures' official synopsis keeps the setup that's defined Sgt. Frog since it began: Keroro, a Keronian sent to Earth as part of an invasion mission, is discovered infiltrating the Hinata household by siblings Fuyuki and Natsumi Hinata. Separated from the rest of the invasion force, he ends up living with the family — still nominally pursuing conquest, mostly getting in the way of it. BN Pictures has not described this as a remake or reboot of specific past storylines, just a new production built around the same starting point.

## How This Differs From the 2026 Movie

Beyond the cast, the two projects serve different purposes: the film functioned as a 20th-anniversary theatrical event with the cast fans have known for two decades, while the TV series is a new, ongoing weekly production aimed at introducing the franchise to television again. Neither has been described by BN Pictures as connected to or building on the other.

## Who Is Making the New Series

Nobuhiro Kondo (*Shonen Ashibe GO! GO! Goma-chan*) is chief director, with Satohiko Sano directing at BN Pictures, animation production handled with Bridge and Aisle. Toshimitsu Takeuchi is overseeing series composition and Hatsue Nakayama is character designer. Mine Yoshizaki's original manga, still running in Kadokawa's Comic Ace, remains the source material.

## What Happens Before the October Premiere

BN Pictures has an advance screening scheduled for September 26 at United Cinemas Toyosu, showing episodes 1 and 2 with a talk event featuring all five new cast members. The studio has also said further details are coming September 14. Whatever's announced then hasn't happened yet — treat anything reported before that date as incomplete.

## What We Still Don't Know

BN Pictures hasn't detailed episode-specific plot content, a full supporting cast beyond the five platoon leads, or how closely the new series will track the original manga's early chapters versus telling new stories. The September 14 announcement is the next confirmed point where more of that could surface.

*Keroro Gunso☆* joins a run of anime franchises returning to Japanese TV this season — Dragon Ball Super: Beerus [premieres the same general window](/articles/dragon-ball-super-beerus-october-11-premiere/), for anyone tracking what else is landing this fall.
