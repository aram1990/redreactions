Dexter Morgan will return on **Friday, October 30, 2026**. Paramount+ has set the Season 2 premiere date for *Dexter: Resurrection* on the Premium Plan and released the first teaser, while a new official synopsis puts Michael C. Hall's antihero between two very different killers in New York.

## The Teaser: An Eye Exam With a Twist

The roughly 30-second tease is built around an optometrist visit. As the doctor cycles through lenses asking Dexter whether his vision is "better before or better now," the imagery intercuts with glimpses of blades and Dexter's familiar tools, before he settles on a new pair of glasses and smirks at the camera. Text reveals the October 30 date alongside the lines "He's Back" and "He's Sharper Than Ever." It's a teaser built around tone and a release date reveal, not a plot preview — nothing about victims, episode order, or which killer does what should be read into the cuts.

## The New Season, According to Paramount

Paramount's official synopsis: Season 2 finds Dexter caught between two killers — one with decades of notoriety and a lifetime of kills behind him, and a younger, newer face terrorizing New York in ways no one has seen. Showrunner Clyde Phillips has also described Dexter working through a midlife crisis of his own this season, alongside Harrison's continued pursuit of justice as part of the show's father-son thread.

Phillips told Entertainment Weekly the season picks up roughly three years after Season 1, aging Harrison (Jack Alcott) to 21 and old enough to join the NYPD — a jump Phillips has framed as a deliberate way to put father and son on more equal, and more complicated, footing.

## Who's Playing the Two Killers

Brian Cox joins as a series regular playing the semi-retired **New York Ripper**, a killer whose reputation precedes him. Dan Stevens also joins as a series regular playing the **Five Borough Killer**, a newer threat working across New York's boroughs. Paramount hasn't detailed how directly these two figures intersect with Dexter's own methods, so treat their exact roles in the season as unconfirmed beyond those introductions.

Michael C. Hall returns as Dexter, with Jack Alcott back as Harrison. Also returning: Uma Thurman, Ntare Guma Mbaho Mwine, Kadia Saraf, Dominic Fumusa, Desmond Harrington, and James Remar. Bokeem Woodbine and Nona Parker Johnson join Cox and Stevens as new series regulars. Krysten Ritter and Gabriel Luna are both confirmed in guest roles — Ritter returning to a part introduced in Season 1, and Luna new to the season.

## Behind the Camera

Clyde Phillips returns as showrunner and executive producer, alongside Michael C. Hall, Scott Reynolds, Marcos Siega, Tony Hernandez, and Lilly Burns among the season's other credited executive producers. Production comes from Paramount Television Studios and Counterpart Studios. The premiere also lands during the *Dexter* franchise's 20th anniversary — the original series debuted in 2006 — though Paramount hasn't built the season's marketing specifically around that milestone beyond noting the timing.

## What's Still Unconfirmed

Paramount hasn't announced whether new episodes roll out weekly or in another format, hasn't detailed how the New York Ripper and Five Borough Killer's stories actually intersect with Dexter's, and hasn't said anything about the season being a final chapter for the character. For now, this is Season 2 of an ongoing series, not a farewell.
