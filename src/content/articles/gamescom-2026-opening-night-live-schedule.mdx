> **Update, August 31, 2026:** Opening Night Live concluded on August 25. This page preserves the pre-show schedule for historical reference; for Red Reactions' follow-up coverage, see our [Fable Gamescom gameplay breakdown](/articles/fable-gameplay-breakdown-albion-combat-explained/), [Fable game-length report](/articles/how-long-is-fable-game-length/), [Persona 4 Revival demo analysis](/articles/persona-4-revival-release-date-prime-time-gameplay/) and [Rainbow Six Tactics reveal](/articles/rainbow-six-tactics-revealed-single-player-strategy-game/).

Opening Night Live took place on **Tuesday, August 25**, hosted by Geoff Keighley alongside Eefje "sjokz" Depoortere. Gamescom's 2026 advance press material confirmed the pair would front the showcase, which featured announcements, world premieres and updates from across the games industry.

The **main broadcast began at 11:00 a.m. PDT / 2:00 p.m. EDT / 7:00 p.m. BST / 8:00 p.m. CEST**, with a 30-minute pre-show beginning at **10:30 a.m. PDT / 1:30 p.m. EDT / 6:30 p.m. BST / 7:30 p.m. CEST**. [GamesRadar's Gamescom schedule](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/events-conferences/gamescom-2026-schedule/) listed the same times.

For viewers in India, the main show began at **11:30 p.m. IST**. In Japan, it started at **3:00 a.m. JST on Wednesday, August 26**.

Gamescom itself ran **August 26 through August 30** at Koelnmesse in Cologne. According to [Gamescom's official 2026 information](https://latam.gamescom.global/en/gamescom-reaches-new-milestone-all-available-areas-for-2026-booked-up/), August 26 was the trade visitor and media day.

A few Opening Night Live appearances had been made public in advance. Gamescom had confirmed updates involving **METRO 2039** and **The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Songs of the Past**. GamesRadar also reported that **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** would appear with game director Naoki Hamaguchi.

That still left plenty under wraps. Opening Night Live is built around trailers, world premieres, gameplay reveals and surprise announcements, so the complete lineup was not known before the broadcast began.

The show streamed through the official Gamescom channels, including YouTube and Twitch.
