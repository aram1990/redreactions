If you're trying to figure out what you actually need to watch before *Puella Magi Madoka Magica the Movie: Walpurgisnacht Rising*, here's the short version: you don't need to watch everything this franchise has ever produced. You need one foundation story and one required sequel.

## The Short Answer: What to Watch Before Walpurgisnacht Rising

**Shortest route:**
1. *Puella Magi Madoka Magica* (TV series)
2. *Rebellion*
3. *Walpurgisnacht Rising*

**Alternative movie route:**
1. *Beginnings*
2. *Eternal*
3. *Rebellion*
4. *Walpurgisnacht Rising*

Pick one foundation, not both. The 12-episode TV series and the *Beginnings*/*Eternal* film pair tell the same original story — you don't need to watch the show and then watch the recap films covering the same ground.

## Route 1: The 12-Episode TV Series

*Puella Magi Madoka Magica* originally aired as a 12-episode TV series, and it's the franchise's original telling of Madoka Kaname, Homura Akemi and the rest of the main cast's story. If you want the version built for weekly, episodic pacing — and don't mind slightly older 2011 TV production values — this is the traditional entry point, and it's the version most commonly available on streaming.

## Route 2: Beginnings + Eternal

*Puella Magi Madoka Magica the Movie Part 1: Beginnings* and *Part 2: Eternal* are compilation films that retell the same 12-episode story in a condensed, theatrical format rather than continuing it. They're not sequels to the TV series and they don't add a new ending — think of them as the same story restructured for a two-film theatrical release, with somewhat higher production polish in places. If you'd rather watch two features than twelve episodes, this route gets you to the same place Route 1 does.

Either route is a legitimate way to establish the foundation. Neither is required on top of the other.

## Why Rebellion Is Required

*Puella Magi Madoka Magica the Movie -Rebellion-* isn't a recap like *Beginnings* and *Eternal* — it's the franchise's original sequel story, picking up after the TV series/compilation-film ending and moving the narrative forward into genuinely new territory. This is the one film in the lineup you can't treat as optional or skippable if *Walpurgisnacht Rising* is your goal: Aniplex's own official materials describe *Walpurgisnacht Rising* as the legitimate, direct continuation of *Rebellion* specifically, not of the TV series or the compilation films on their own. Skipping *Rebellion* means walking into *Walpurgisnacht Rising* without the story it's actually continuing.

## Where Walpurgisnacht Rising Fits

Without spoiling *Rebellion*'s own ending, here's the shape of it: *Walpurgisnacht Rising* picks up from where *Rebellion* left its story, rather than jumping ahead or restarting with a new premise. It's being positioned by Aniplex as the next chapter built directly on *Rebellion*'s unresolved threads, not a standalone spin-off you could understand in isolation. If you've seen *Rebellion*, you have what you need going in; if you haven't, nothing about *Walpurgisnacht Rising*'s marketing is designed to work as a fresh starting point.

One practical note for newcomers: recent official promotional material for *Walpurgisnacht Rising* has leaned into imagery and beats that connect fairly directly to *Rebellion*'s own ending. If you haven't seen *Rebellion* yet, it's worth being a little cautious about how closely you look at new trailers and PVs before you get there — without getting into specifics, some of that marketing assumes you already know how *Rebellion* ends.

## Do You Need to Watch Magia Record?

No. *Magia Record: Puella Magi Madoka Magica Side Story* is exactly what its name says — a side story, originally built around a mobile game, that exists alongside the mainline film continuity rather than inside it. It's not a prerequisite for *Rebellion* or *Walpurgisnacht Rising*, and skipping it won't leave you missing information the films assume you have. If you enjoy the franchise and want more of it after finishing the mainline story, it's there — but it's genuinely optional, not required homework.

## What You Can Skip for Now

Beyond Magia Record, the wider Madoka Magica franchise includes additional manga spin-offs, other mobile games, and assorted side material that have accumulated over more than a decade. None of it is necessary to understand *Rebellion* or *Walpurgisnacht Rising*. If your goal right now is specifically "catch up before Walpurgisnacht Rising," the TV series (or Beginnings/Eternal) plus Rebellion is genuinely the complete list — treat everything else as optional expansion you can explore later if you want more, not a checklist you need to clear first.

## TV Series or Recap Movies — Which Route Is Better?

Neither route is objectively correct, and the choice mostly comes down to preference and access. The TV series is the original version and the one most viewers have historically started with, but it does carry some visibly older animation in places. *Beginnings* and *Eternal* compress the same story into two features with a more polished theatrical presentation, which can be an easier watch if you'd rather not commit to twelve episodes. Both routes end up putting you in the same place going into *Rebellion*, so pick whichever format fits how you like to watch.

## Where to Watch Madoka Magica Legally

The original 12-episode TV series is available on Crunchyroll. *Beginnings*, *Eternal* and *Rebellion* have been available through Netflix, with rental or purchase options also available on services like Prime Video and Apple TV depending on your region — availability can shift, so it's worth checking current listings on those platforms directly. *Walpurgisnacht Rising* is currently a theatrical release in Japan and North America with no streaming or home-release date announced yet, consistent with how Aniplex has handled prior mainline films in this franchise.

## Bottom Line

Pick one foundation — the TV series or *Beginnings*/*Eternal* — then watch *Rebellion*, which is the one film in this lineup you genuinely can't skip before *Walpurgisnacht Rising*. Magia Record and the rest of the franchise's spin-off material are optional extras, not prerequisites. That's the whole list.
