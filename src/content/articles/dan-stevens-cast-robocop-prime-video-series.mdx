RoboCop has found its Alex Murphy. [Deadline reports](https://deadline.com/2026/08/dan-stevens-cast-robocop-prime-video-series-1237049398/) that Dan Stevens has been tapped to star as the fallen police officer and his cyborg alter ego in Prime Video's upcoming **RoboCop** series. [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/robocop-tv-series-cast-dan-stevens-1236842597/) and The Hollywood Reporter subsequently confirmed the casting.

The news gives Amazon MGM Studios' new take on the 1987 sci-fi franchise its most important cast member just over a month after Prime Video ordered the project to series. The show has an **eight-episode order**, with Peter Ocko serving as writer, executive producer and showrunner.

No premiere date has been announced, and there is no indication yet that filming has started.

## Dan Stevens Will Play Both Alex Murphy and RoboCop

Stevens isn't joining the series as a new character built around RoboCop. He's taking on the franchise's defining role: **Alex Murphy**, the police officer whose consciousness is placed inside a powerful robotic body after his death.

The official premise keeps that basic idea intact. A major technology corporation persuades a city to put a powerful robot on its police force, only for that machine to carry the consciousness of the fallen Murphy.

That setup closely echoes the foundation of Paul Verhoeven's 1987 film without telling us exactly how closely the series will follow its story. Amazon MGM has not established whether this version shares continuity with the original movies or the 2014 remake, so it is safer to treat the show as a new television interpretation rather than assume a direct connection to either continuity.

Stevens is a strong genre choice for the role. Beyond **Downton Abbey**, he led FX's surreal Marvel series **Legion** and has appeared in projects including **The Guest**, **Abigail** and **Godzilla x Kong: The New Empire**. RoboCop asks its lead to sell both the human tragedy of Murphy and the deliberately mechanical presence of the character after his transformation, making the casting one of the biggest indicators yet of what kind of performance the series may be aiming for.

## James Wan and the Original RoboCop Writers Are Involved

The creative team also gives the project a direct link back to RoboCop's history.

James Wan, Michael Clear and Rob Hackett executive produce through **Blumhouse Atomic Monster**, while Danielle Bozzone serves as co-executive producer. **Ed Neumeier and Michael Miner**, who co-wrote the original 1987 film, are executive producers as well. Amazon MGM Studios is producing the series.

Their involvement does not mean the show is a continuation of the original timeline, but it does put two of the creators behind the first film inside the new production.

Wan has previously emphasized how relevant RoboCop's themes remain, particularly its ideas about technology, identity and corporate power. Those subjects were central to the original film's mix of violent sci-fi action and satire, and they arguably give a modern RoboCop adaptation plenty to work with without simply recreating the 1987 movie scene for scene.

## RoboCop Has Been Moving Toward TV for Years

Amazon's interest in reviving RoboCop dates back to the period following its acquisition of MGM. The property emerged as one of several MGM titles being considered for new film and television projects, and the current series gradually took shape with Ocko and Wan's team attached before Prime Video formally handed it an eight-episode order in July 2026.

The franchise itself has already moved between formats several times. Peter Weller originated the role in **RoboCop** in 1987 and returned for **RoboCop 2** in 1990. Robert Burke took over for **RoboCop 3**, while Joel Kinnaman played Murphy in the 2014 movie reboot. RoboCop has also previously made the jump to television in both live-action and animated form.

For now, Stevens is the only major cast member announced for Prime Video's version. There is **no confirmed release date**, and further casting and production details remain under wraps.

But landing Alex Murphy is a major step. After years of development, Prime Video's RoboCop series finally has the man who will wear the badge — and eventually the armor.
