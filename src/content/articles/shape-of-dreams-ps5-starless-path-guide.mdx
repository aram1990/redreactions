*Shape of Dreams* is now available on PlayStation 5, and the console launch arrives with considerably more than a new platform. Developer Lizard Smoothie has released **The Starless Path**, its first-anniversary update and what the studio calls the game's largest update yet, adding a ninth playable Traveler, a new story progression system, a new ending and tougher boss behavior.

The PS5 version supports solo play and four-player online co-op, with cross-play connecting PlayStation players to PC and other console lobbies. Here is what is actually included at launch — and how the game's build system works if you are entering the Rapids for the first time.

## What Is Shape of Dreams?

*Shape of Dreams* is an action roguelite built around fast, ability-driven combat in a shifting dream world called the Rapids. Each run starts with one of nine Travelers, but the character choice is only the foundation of a build.

The game separates abilities and modifiers into two parts. **Memories** are the active abilities you equip, while **Essences** alter how those abilities function. An Essence can make a single-target attack chain to nearby enemies, for example, or reduce its cooldown when it lands. Lizard Smoothie says the game now contains more than 200 Memories and Essences.

Each Traveler also begins with two defining traits: an active **Traveler Memory** and a passive **Identity Memory**. Players choose between two options in each slot before a run, changing the build's starting point before random upgrades enter the picture.

## Solo, Four-Player Co-op and Cross-Play

The complete game can be played solo, but online parties support up to four players. Co-op does not lock anyone into a fixed tank, support or damage role. Instead, independent builds can create combinations — one player might freeze a group while another deals extra damage to frozen targets, or pull enemies together for a teammate's area attack.

Cross-play is active on PS5 at launch, and Lizard Smoothie says PlayStation lobbies are unified with PC and other console players. A PlayStation Plus subscription is required for online play on PS5; solo play remains available without it.

## Everything New in The Starless Path

The headline addition is **Cetus**, a ranged frost tank and the game's ninth Traveler. Cetus can control groups with ice while maintaining shields for himself and his teammates, giving parties a defensive option without turning the game into a rigid role-based system.

The update also adds **The Way of Stars**, a progression system that connects each Traveler's story through a constellation. Reaching deeper parts of the Rapids unlocks hidden episodes and illustrations, leading to a new ending and what the developer describes as the game's toughest final boss.

Existing world bosses receive new secondary attack patterns at higher difficulties. That is a mechanical change rather than simply a larger health pool: returning bosses can behave differently as the challenge increases.

Finally, **Fallen Stars and Faded Memories** expands the Identity Memory system. A Traveler's character-specific passive can now be replaced with one of six Universal Identity Memories, opening another route for builds that do not fit the character's default identity.

## Does Progress Carry Between Runs?

Yes. Each Traveler has a Constellation that preserves progression after a run ends, and *The Starless Path* adds new Stars to slot into it. Individual runs still reset in roguelite fashion, but long-term character growth is not lost when a descent into the Rapids ends.

## PS5 Release, Price and Online Requirements

*Shape of Dreams* launched on PS5 on **September 17, 2026**, published by NEOWIZ. The U.S. PlayStation Store lists a standard price of **$24.99**, with a temporary PlayStation Plus discount shown at the time of publication; regional prices and promotions vary.

The store lists one local player, optional online play for up to four players, Remote Play support and PS5/PlayStation Portal cloud streaming for Premium subscribers. Online multiplayer requires PlayStation Plus. The launch version also includes a limited launch-bonus costume for Lacerta, according to the official store listing.

## What Is Confirmed — and What Is Not

The official launch materials confirm nine Travelers, more than 200 Memories and Essences, four-player online co-op, cross-play, persistent Constellation progression and all of the *Starless Path* additions above. They do not promise that progression itself transfers between platforms, so cross-play should not be read as confirmation of cross-save.
