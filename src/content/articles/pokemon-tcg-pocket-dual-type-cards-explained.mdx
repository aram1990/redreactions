Pokémon TCG Pocket is getting dual-type cards for the first time, and The Pokémon Company revealed the mechanic at PokémonXP this weekend with a concrete example: Mega Mewtwo X ex, a card that's simultaneously Psychic and Fighting. It arrives in October alongside a new series of expansions, and it's a bigger structural change than a typical card drop — every Pokémon in the game so far has belonged to exactly one type.

## What's actually new here

Pocket has run on single-type cards since launch. A Psychic Pokémon was Psychic, full stop, and that determined what Energy it could run, what it was weak to, and which Trainer support and archetypes it fit into. Dual-type cards break that one-to-one relationship for the first time. Mega Mewtwo X ex carries both types on the same card, and its art reflects that directly, split down the middle to show both typings at once.

The physical Pokémon Trading Card Game has flirted with multi-type identities in various forms over the years, so the underlying idea isn't unprecedented for the franchise. It's new specifically to Pocket, which has run its own simplified ruleset since it launched.

## What Mega Mewtwo X ex actually does

The confirmed facts: it's Psychic and Fighting, runs on Psychic Energy, and is weak to Dark-type attacks. Early coverage of the reveal, including Pokémon GO Hub's breakdown of the card, describes its dual typing working as a genuine mechanical advantage rather than just cosmetic flavor — dealing bonus damage against Pokémon that are weak to either Psychic or Fighting attacks, effectively doubling the range of matchups where it hits harder. The Pokémon Company hasn't published a full stat line (HP, exact attack damage, retreat cost) as of this writing, so treat those specifics as unconfirmed until the card is live and readable in-game.

## What it could mean for deck building

This is where it gets interesting for anyone who actually plays Pocket rather than just collects. A card that qualifies as two types at once could, depending on how Pocket's Trainer cards and support effects check typing, plug into two archetypes' worth of synergy instead of one. If Psychic-boosting support cards and Fighting-boosting support cards both recognize Mega Mewtwo X ex as eligible, that's a meaningfully different deck-building space than anything currently possible in Pocket.

None of that is confirmed as a blanket rule yet — it depends on exactly how The Pokémon Company implements type-checking for existing Trainer and Supporter effects, and that hasn't been fully detailed. What's real right now is the card itself and its stated typing; what happens to the wider ruleset around it is the part worth watching once October's expansion is actually playable.

## The rest of the October reveal

The same announcement confirmed First Partner Pokémon are getting their own ex cards, starting with Piplup ex and Fuecoco ex, with more starter Pokémon expected to follow. That's a separate addition from dual-typing — it's about giving starter Pokémon the ex treatment, not about multi-typing — bundled into the same October rollout and the same new expansion series. The Pokémon Company hasn't named that expansion series yet.

This drops about six weeks after Team Rocket's Ambition, which launched August 26. That was a themed content expansion built around Team Rocket cards; October's update is a change to how cards themselves can be built, which is a different kind of addition entirely. It also lands amid a broader run of Pokémon news beyond the card game, including [Disney+ picking up global distribution for the animated series Pokémon Tales](/articles/disney-pokemon-distribution-deal-aardman-sirfetchd-pichu/).

## What's still unconfirmed

The Pokémon Company hasn't released a full card list for October, hasn't named the new expansion series, and hasn't published Mega Mewtwo X ex's complete stat line or confirmed exactly how existing type-based Trainer cards will interact with a Pokémon that qualifies as two types simultaneously. Mega Mewtwo X ex is only the first dual-type card The Pokémon Company has shown — the more interesting question now is how far they take the mechanic across the rest of October's cards.
