*Overgeared*'s anime adaptation — officially titled *Tempal ~The Power of Items~* in Japan — dropped its third PV on September 13, 2026, and it changes more than the footage on screen: two new cast members, the ending theme, and a confirmed early Prime Video start in Japan are all new here.

## What's New in This PV

- **Third official trailer**, titled 「第3弾PV」 (PV 3), uploaded by Pony Canyon's official anime channel
- Two newly announced cast members: **Yuki Murata as Irene** and **Yurika Hirayama as Isabel**
- **Ending theme "Ray of Emotion"** by virtual singer **TAO**, previewed in this PV for the first time
- Confirmed distribution update: **Prime Video begins streaming in Japan on September 27, 2026 (Sundays, 23:30 JST)** — five days ahead of the **October 2, 2026 TV broadcast (Fridays, 23:30 JST on TOKYO MX and BS11)**
- Opening theme confirmed as **"SATISFY" by Re:name**

## What's Shown vs. What's Confirmed

The PV itself features new battle footage and, per coverage of the reveal, scenes suggesting a closer emotional connection developing between protagonist Greed and Irene. That framing comes from how the trailer is edited and described in the accompanying press coverage — it's a reasonable read of what's shown, not an official plot confirmation. Neither Irene's nor Isabel's full role in the story, their relationship to the existing Tsedaka Guild cast, or any romantic outcome has been spelled out by official character or synopsis material. Treat the romance angle as visible in the trailer's editing, not as a confirmed story beat.

## Japan Streaming Now Starts Before Japan TV

This is the most concrete new information in the update: Prime Video's Japanese service will carry *Tempal* starting **September 27**, a full five days before the terrestrial broadcast begins October 2. That's a genuine schedule change worth flagging specifically because it inverts the usual order — streaming ahead of TV, rather than following it. This applies to **Prime Video in Japan specifically**; it is not confirmation of a simultaneous or early global Prime Video launch.

## International Streaming Stays Separate

*Overgeared* was separately announced for **Crunchyroll**, confirmed at Crunchyroll's Anime Expo 2026 industry showcase back in July, targeting the same October 2026 window. Nothing in this third-PV update changes that. The September 27 Prime Video date is a Japan-specific early-access window; Crunchyroll's own international release timing should be checked against Crunchyroll's own announcements rather than assumed to match Japan's schedule.

## What Hasn't Changed

The core production credits remain the same as prior announcements: animation by **J.C.STAFF**, director **Ayako Kono**, series composition by **Kenta Ihara**, character designs by **Ryosuke Tanigawa**, and music by **Yoshiaki Fujisawa**. The October 2 TV broadcast date and TOKYO MX/BS11 slot are unchanged from earlier reveals — this update adds cast, music, and the Prime Video timing on top of what was already known.

## Bottom Line

The third *Overgeared* PV isn't just new footage — it locks in Irene and Isabel as confirmed cast additions, reveals the ending theme for the first time, and confirms Japan's Prime Video stream starts a full five days before the TV broadcast. International viewers should still look to Crunchyroll's own announcements for their release window, not this Japan-specific Prime Video date.
