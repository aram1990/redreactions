*Mobile Suit Gundam RG XARX-ZERO* now has a launch window: **April 2027, in Japan and North America**, confirmed September 14, 2026. It's the anime half of Bandai Namco's new **RG Project** — a shared universe built alongside the action game *GUNDAM ROGUE ORBIT*, which Red Reactions has [already covered in depth](/articles/gundam-rogue-orbit-trailer-breakdown/).

## What's actually confirmed

- **Launch window:** April 2027, confirmed for **Japan and North America** specifically. No other regions have received a release window as of this announcement — don't assume a simultaneous worldwide launch.
- **Director and series composer:** Kenji Kamiyama (*Ghost in the Shell: Stand Alone Complex*), who also created the RG Project's shared setting alongside SF setting supervisor Toh Enjoe.
- **Animation studio:** SOLA ANIMATION. **Production:** Bandai Namco Filmworks. **Planning:** Sunrise.
- **Protagonist:** Ray Azumi, a war orphan, voiced by Takeo Otsuka.
- **Main mobile suit:** Gundam ZERO (model designation XARX-0).
- **Timeline:** the **After Apocalypse (A.A.)** calendar, with the anime's story beginning in **A.A. 45**.

## What RG XARX-ZERO is actually about

The RG Project's shared premise starts with a single event: an interstellar object entering the solar system delivers humanity three transformative technologies — gravity control, faster-than-light communication, and cognitive materialization — before turning hostile, triggering a self-replication system that spawns aggressive, silicon-based lifeforms known as **the Apocalypse**. That crisis is the "After Apocalypse" the timeline is named for, and *XARX-ZERO* is set decades into the world that crisis created, following Ray Azumi as a war orphan piloting the Gundam ZERO.

## How RG XARX-ZERO connects to Rogue Orbit

Both projects share the same After Apocalypse setting and originate from the same creative team — Kamiyama and Toh Enjoe built the world both stories take place in. Bandai Namco's own materials are explicit that the anime and game are designed to work independently: each stands on its own without requiring the other. Experiencing both is framed as adding depth to the shared universe rather than being necessary to follow either one. Our own [Rogue Orbit trailer breakdown](/articles/gundam-rogue-orbit-trailer-breakdown/) covers Bandai Namco's official framing of the game as a newcomer-friendly entry point in more detail — that same design philosophy applies here.

One thing worth being precise about: Bandai Namco has not published an official statement quantifying exactly how much time separates *XARX-ZERO*'s A.A. 45 setting from *Rogue Orbit*'s own story. Some secondary coverage has floated an approximate gap, but until Bandai Namco confirms a specific figure in its own materials, treat any such number as unverified.

## Why the anime and game have distinct search intents

*Rogue Orbit* is a newcomer-friendly action game — a Mobile Suit customization and combat title releasing March 5, 2027, playable without any prior Gundam knowledge. *XARX-ZERO* is a television anime with its own director, cast and story, releasing the following month. They share a universe, not a plot — someone searching for the game's combat mechanics and someone searching for the anime's broadcast schedule are looking for two different things, which is why this is a separate page from our Rogue Orbit coverage rather than a section inside it.

## What remains unknown

Bandai Namco hasn't announced North American streaming or broadcast platform details, a Japanese network, an exact April release date, or an episode count. Treat the April 2027 window as a confirmed month, not yet a confirmed day, and don't assume any particular platform will carry the series in North America until Bandai Namco says so directly.

## The bottom line

*RG XARX-ZERO* is now dated, staffed and named — April 2027 in Japan and North America, directed by Kenji Kamiyama, starring Ray Azumi's Gundam ZERO in a world built to run alongside *Rogue Orbit* without depending on it. The two projects share a universe by design; whether that pays off is still a year and a half away.
