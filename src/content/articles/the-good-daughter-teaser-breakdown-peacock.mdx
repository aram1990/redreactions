Peacock has released the first teaser for *The Good Daughter*, its six-episode limited series adapting Karin Slaughter's bestselling novel — and it centers on exactly the relationship the book is built around: two sisters, one small town, and a single night of violence they've spent twenty years trying to leave behind.

## What the Teaser Confirms

Peacock's official synopsis frames *The Good Daughter* as "a suspenseful thriller wrapped in a deeply emotional story about love, family and resilience." Sisters **Samantha** (Rose Byrne) and **Charlotte "Charlie" Quinn** (Meghann Fahy) have spent two decades trying to piece their lives back together after a childhood home invasion ended in their mother's murder. When a new act of violence hits their hometown of Pikeville, Charlotte — now a lawyer like her father — becomes the first witness on the scene, pulling both sisters back into memories and family secrets they'd rather have left buried.

That dual-timeline structure — the original tragedy and the new attack that reopens it — is the engine of both the novel and, based on the teaser and Peacock's official materials, the series itself.

## Karin Slaughter Adapting Her Own Novel

*The Good Daughter* is written and executive produced by Karin Slaughter herself, who adapted her 2017 novel for television. That matters for how confidently anything here can be reported: when a novelist adapts their own book, plot details from interviews and official synopses carry more weight than usual speculation about "how faithful" a TV version will be, since the same author controls both versions.

Slaughter has said the two-week writing stretch that produced the scripts let her stay close to the novel's core relationship. She's credited Byrne and Fahy with capturing "the sister relationship — all the history, heartache, and joy — wrapped inside this puzzle of a mystery." That's Slaughter's own framing for the adaptation, not outside praise.

## Rose Byrne, Meghann Fahy and the Cast Peacock Has Confirmed

Officially confirmed cast includes:

- **Rose Byrne** as Samantha Quinn
- **Meghann Fahy** as Charlotte "Charlie" Quinn
- **Brendan Gleeson** as Rusty Quinn, the sisters' defense-attorney father
- **Olivia Williams** as the sisters' mother
- **Harper Steele**, **Michael Dorman**, **Audrey Grace Marshall** and **Drew Cheek** in additional roles

Steph Green, who directed episodes of *Watchmen*, directs the series. Bruna Papandrea, Steve Hutensky, Casey Haver and Rose Byrne herself are among the executive producers alongside Slaughter.

## What Belongs to the Novel, Not (Yet) the Show

Readers of Slaughter's novel will recognize the setup immediately, but the teaser and Peacock's official materials don't confirm how closely the series will track the book's later plot turns — who the new attacker is, how the original home invasion connects to it, or how the sisters' present-day relationship resolves. None of that is confirmed for the adaptation yet, and nothing here should be read as a guarantee that TV events will unfold exactly as they do on the page. Treat the shared premise as confirmed; treat the rest of the novel's plot as background, not a spoiler preview of the show.

## Why This One Stands Out

*The Good Daughter* arrives in a crowded fall thriller lineup, but the teaser leans on two things that separate it from a generic true-crime-adjacent drama: a novelist writing her own adaptation, and two leads — Byrne and Fahy — playing sisters rather than strangers thrown together by a case. Fahy told Vanity Fair she hopes viewers come away from the show with something beyond the darkness of its premise: "What people take from watching *The Good Daughter* is just that they have hope and that they are encouraged to walk toward their trauma." That's a notably different pitch than most "small town, buried secret" thrillers make in their marketing, and it's worth taking at face value rather than dismissing as press-tour framing, since it lines up with how Slaughter has described the adaptation's focus on the sisters' relationship over pure mystery mechanics.

## Release: November 12, All Six Episodes at Once

*The Good Daughter* premieres on Peacock in the US on **November 12, 2026**, with all six episodes released the same day rather than a weekly rollout. Sky and NOW carry the series in the UK and Ireland; Sky's own announcement confirms the series is "coming this November" without specifying an exact UK date beyond that, so treat November 12 as the confirmed US date and UK/Ireland timing as "November 2026" until Sky specifies further.
