**EAT-MAN The Over Order**, a brand-new anime project based on Akihito Yoshitomi's sci-fi manga *EAT-MAN*, was announced on September 10, 2026 — the franchise's first new anime production in **28 years**, since *EAT-MAN '98* aired in late 1998. The teaser visual's own Japanese text translates to "Complete new anime production decided" ("完全新作アニメ化決定"), and the announcement confirms this is an original story rather than a straight remake of the 1990s series.

## Why "28 years" is accurate here

The original *EAT-MAN* TV anime aired January–March 1997, followed by the sequel series *EAT-MAN '98* that October through December. *The Over Order* is the franchise's third anime production overall, and its announcement — 2026 minus 1998 — genuinely lands 28 years after the last one aired. This isn't a round-number approximation; the chronology checks out.

## An original story, not a remake

Yoshitomi isn't just licensing the property out — he's credited with the **original work, script, and original design concept** for *The Over Order*, with Satoshi Hirayama adapting those designs for animation under director Shunji Oga at Studio CAB. That's the clearest signal this is new material built by the creator himself rather than a retelling of the 1997–1998 story. Red Reactions isn't assuming any specific continuity link between the older anime and this project — nothing in the announcement addresses that directly.

## Masashi Ebara returns as Bolt Crank

**Masashi Ebara**, who voiced protagonist Bolt Crank in the original 1990s anime, is confirmed to reprise the role. Bolt is a mercenary "explorer" with the ability to consume ordinary objects — scrap metal, machinery, nearly anything — and reconstitute them as weapons and tools. The teaser trailer reportedly opens with a message referencing the Heisei era before showing Bolt picking up a screw, declaring he's ready to work, and producing an enormous weapon from consumed metal — a direct callback to the character's core hook for anyone unfamiliar with the original.
