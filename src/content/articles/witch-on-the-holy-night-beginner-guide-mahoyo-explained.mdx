**No, Witch on the Holy Night is not a Fate sequel, and you don't need to watch Fate first.** It's its own story centered on three characters — Aoko, Alice and Soujyuro — created by Kinoko Nasu within the wider family of TYPE-MOON works. ufotable's film adaptation premieres in Japan November 20, 2026, and this guide covers what the story actually is, who these characters are, and exactly how much TYPE-MOON homework you genuinely need before watching.

## What Is Witch on the Holy Night?

**Witch on the Holy Night** — known in Japan as *Mahoutsukai no Yoru*, often shortened to "Mahoyo" — is a visual novel TYPE-MOON released in Japan in April 2012. It was written and scenario-directed by **Kinoko Nasu**, one of TYPE-MOON's two co-founders, and it's a significant work for him personally: an early draft of the story was the first thing he ever wrote, back in the mid-1990s, years before Tsukihime or Fate/stay night existed.

The story is set in the late 1980s in the town of Misaki. Aoko Aozaki, heir to a family of modern mages, is abruptly pulled into the world of magecraft and ends up living with — and learning from — Alice Kuonji, a witch with a reputation for staying isolated. Soujyuro Shizuki, an ordinary boy new to the area, gets drawn into their orbit and into events neither he nor the reader fully understands at first.

## Meet Aoko, Alice and Soujyuro

**Aoko Aozaki** (voiced by Haruka Tomatsu) is a high schooler who suddenly becomes the heir to her family's magecraft tradition and has to learn the basics from scratch. **Alice Kuonji** (voiced by Kana Hanazawa) is a young magus who lives in seclusion and is locally rumored to be a witch — she becomes Aoko's teacher. **Soujyuro Shizuki** (voiced by Yūsuke Kobayashi) is a newcomer to town whose ordinary life intersects with both of theirs. All three actors are reprising their roles from the 2022–2023 remastered version of the visual novel for ufotable's film.

## What Is the Original Visual Novel?

The 2012 original was a Windows-only release. TYPE-MOON later gave it a full remaster: fully voiced, rebuilt in Full HD, and released internationally on Nintendo Switch and PlayStation 4 on December 8, 2022, followed by a Steam release on December 14, 2023. That remastered version is the one carrying English text and English-accessible releases today, and it's the version whose cast is returning for the film.

Structurally, it's worth knowing that *Witch on the Holy Night* is a linear kinetic novel — unlike Fate/stay night's branching routes, there's one story with no player-chosen paths. That said, neither Aniplex nor TYPE-MOON has detailed exactly how much of that story this specific film adaptation covers, so it's not safe to assume the movie is a complete, one-to-one retelling of the full visual novel just because the source material itself doesn't branch.

## How It Relates to TYPE-MOON, Fate and Tsukihime

This is the part that intimidates newcomers, so here's the direct version: TYPE-MOON's works — Tsukihime, Fate/stay night, The Garden of Sinners, and Witch on the Holy Night among them — share a creator and some overlapping concepts, but they aren't one single continuous story you need to consume in order. Most of them stand on their own.

Witch on the Holy Night has one concrete, specific connection worth knowing: it functions as a prequel of sorts to **Tsukihime**, showing the younger years of Aoko Aozaki before she becomes an early mentor figure to Tsukihime's protagonist, Shiki Tohno. That's a real, direct link — but it's also the extent of what you need to know going in. You don't need to have played Tsukihime to follow Aoko, Alice and Soujyuro's story here; the connection matters more if you go on to play Tsukihime afterward and recognize an older Aoko in a new context.

## Why ufotable Is Adapting It

ufotable is the studio behind the *Fate/stay night: Unlimited Blade Works* and *Heaven's Feel* adaptations, as well as *The Garden of Sinners*. Witch on the Holy Night is their latest TYPE-MOON project, continuing a working relationship between the studio and TYPE-MOON that goes back over a decade. That history is one reason the film has drawn attention beyond just TYPE-MOON's existing fanbase — ufotable's involvement is treated as a strong signal of visual and production quality on its own.

## What the Movie Is Confirmed to Cover — and What Is Still Unknown

Confirmed: the film retells the core Aoko/Alice/Soujyuro story from the visual novel, with the original 2022–2023 remaster's voice cast returning. Not yet confirmed: the exact scope of what's included or trimmed, and whether any material is being restructured for a single film's runtime. Treat any claim about "the complete story" or "everything from the VN" as unverified until Aniplex or TYPE-MOON states it directly.

## When the Movie Releases Worldwide

Japan gets it first, on **November 20, 2026**. Crunchyroll and Sony Pictures Entertainment are handling the international theatrical rollout starting January 2027, with the US, Canada and UK opening January 29, and Spain following February 5. For the complete country-by-country schedule, see our [full worldwide release date breakdown](/articles/witch-on-the-holy-night-worldwide-release-dates/).

## Should You Play the Visual Novel First?

You don't have to. The film is being made as its own entry point, and Aoko, Alice and Soujyuro's story doesn't require outside context to follow. If you'd rather read it first, the remastered version on Switch, PS4 or Steam is fully voiced and the most accessible way in. Either order works — this isn't a case where skipping the source spoils anything you'd need for the movie, or vice versa.

## Bottom Line

Witch on the Holy Night is TYPE-MOON's own story, not a piece of Fate homework. It shares a creator and one direct character link with Tsukihime, but you can walk into ufotable's film — or the visual novel — knowing nothing else about TYPE-MOON and still follow exactly what's happening.
